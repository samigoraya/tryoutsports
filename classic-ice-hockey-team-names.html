    What makes a good classic ice hockey team name?

    Classic names lean on tradition, trades and hometown pride. They are short, timeless and look good stitched across a jersey. For ice hockey, that means mixing classic words with the sport itself: Slap Shots, Power Plays and Face Offs all work well.

    • Pair your town with a trade or tradition, like Millers, Pioneers or Railmen.
    • Keep it to two words so it fits a jersey and reads clearly on a scoreboard.
    • Pick a name that will still sound good in ten years.
    • Mix in ice hockey words like Slap Shots, Power Plays or Face Offs to tie the name to the sport.

    Classic words that fit ice hockey

    Classic names mix a describing word with a ice hockey word. Try describing words like Union, Coastal, Summer, Valley, Silver, Dusty, Mountain, Iron, Canyon, Old, next to ice hockey words like Zambonis, Breakaways, Puck Stoppers, Face Offs, Enforcers, Slap Shots, Hat Tricks, Power Plays, Penalty Killers, Wingers. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
