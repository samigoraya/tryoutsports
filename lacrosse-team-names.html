    About lacrosse

    Lacrosse is played with netted sticks used to carry, pass and shoot a hard ball, and its roots lie in Indigenous North American games. Field lacrosse, box lacrosse and the newer sixes format all have their own leagues. It is strongest in the United States, Canada and increasingly in Europe and Asia.

    • Team size10 a side in the field game, 6 in the box and sixes codes
    • Biggest eventsWorld Lacrosse Championship, Premier Lacrosse League, NCAA lacrosse

    What makes a good lacrosse team name?

    Lacrosse is played with netted sticks, and its roots lie in Indigenous North American games. The best lacrosse team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Attackers, Middies or Defenders, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Attackers, Middies or Defenders, so the name clearly belongs to lacrosse.
    • Say it out loud. A good lacrosse team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Match the tone to the team: fierce for competitive squads, funny or cute for social and youth teams.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers, Alliance, Athletic or Sporting. Columbus Crew and Philadelphia Union are built this way, and it works for any sport. Try your town plus Crew for a lacrosse side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For lacrosse that means Attackers, Middies or Defenders, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
