    What makes a good funny curling team name?

    The best funny names turn a word from the sport into a joke: a pun, an odd animal or a snack. If it makes your own team laugh, it works. For curling, that means mixing funny words with the sport itself: Curlers, Sweepers and Skips all work well.

    • Twist a word from your sport into a pun.
    • Add food for an easy laugh: Waffles, Pickles or Nachos.
    • Put a goofy adjective on the front: Sleepy, Soggy or Wobbly.
    • Mix in curling words like Curlers, Sweepers or Skips to tie the name to the sport.

    Funny words that fit curling

    Funny names mix a describing word with a curling word. Try describing words like Clumsy, Late, Confused, Wobbly, Fearless, Mighty, Loud, Juggling, Grumpy, Soggy, next to curling words like Draw Masters, Hammer Holders, Skips, House Holders, Brush Brigade, Rock Rollers, Hog Liners, Sweepers, Curlers, Stone Throwers. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
