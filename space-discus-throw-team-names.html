    What makes a good space discus throw team name?

    Space names borrow rockets, planets and galaxies. They sound fast and futuristic and look great with metallic colors. For discus throw, that means mixing space words with the sport itself: Discus Throwers, Disc Spinners and Spin Masters all work well.

    • Use planets and objects: Comets, Meteors, Rockets or Nebulas.
    • Add a futuristic word like Cosmic, Stellar, Orbital or Quantum.
    • Silver, navy and electric blue match the theme.
    • Mix in discus throw words like Discus Throwers, Disc Spinners or Spin Masters to tie the name to the sport.
