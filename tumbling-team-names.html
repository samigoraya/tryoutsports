    About tumbling

    Tumbling runs a pass of flips and twists along a sprung 25m track, scored on difficulty and execution. Clubs enter squads and score as teams at national meets. Russia, the United States, Great Britain, China and Portugal are among the strongest nations.

    What makes a good tumbling team name?

    Tumbling is a floor tumbling discipline with passes of flips and twists on a springy track. The best tumbling team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Tumblers, Pass Crew or Back Handsprings, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Tumblers, Pass Crew or Back Handsprings, so the name clearly belongs to tumbling.
    • Say it out loud. A good tumbling team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For tumbling that means Tumblers, Pass Crew or Back Handsprings, which tells everyone what you play.
