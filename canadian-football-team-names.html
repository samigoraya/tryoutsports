    About Canadian football

    Canadian football is gridiron played on a wider, longer field with twelve players a side and only three downs to make ten yards. That makes for more passing and more open play than the American game. The Canadian Football League and university football carry the sport.

    • Team size12 on the field, with three downs
    • Biggest eventsGrey Cup, CFL season

    What makes a good Canadian football team name?

    Canadian football is gridiron played on a wider, longer field with twelve players, three downs and a big end zone. The best Canadian football team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Rouges, Receivers or Linemen, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Rouges, Receivers or Linemen, so the name clearly belongs to Canadian football.
    • Say it out loud. A good Canadian football team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
    • A word from the sport itself. For Canadian football that means Rouges, Receivers or Linemen, which tells everyone what you play.
    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Alliance, Syndicate, Collective or Brigade. Try your town plus Crew for a Canadian football side.
