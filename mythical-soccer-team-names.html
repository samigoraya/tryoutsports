    What makes a good mythical soccer team name?

    Mythical names draw on dragons, phoenixes, titans and legends. They feel epic and make great logos. For soccer, that means mixing mythical words with the sport itself: Strikers, Midfielders and Defenders all work well.

    • Pick a creature with a strong silhouette: Dragons, Griffins or Phoenixes.
    • Add a mythic word like Ancient, Immortal, Eternal or Legend.
    • Borrow from the myths of your own region for a local twist.
    • Mix in soccer words like Strikers, Midfielders or Defenders to tie the name to the sport.

    Mythical words that fit soccer

    Mythical names mix a describing word with a soccer word. Try describing words like Fabled, Ancient, Celestial, Crystal, Elder, Sacred, Enchanted, Thunder, Arcane, Emerald, next to soccer words like Crossbars, Super Subs, Rovers, Wingers, Counter Attackers, Goalposts, Strikers, Dribblers, Playmakers, Box Crashers. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
