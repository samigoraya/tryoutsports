    About parkour

    Parkour moves through an environment using vaults, climbs, jumps and precision landings, now contested in speed and freestyle formats. Traceurs almost always train in a crew, and crew names are a big part of the culture. France is the birthplace, with strong scenes in Great Britain, Russia, Japan, the United States and Denmark.

    What makes a good parkour team name?

    Parkour is about moving through spaces with vaults, jumps and climbs. The best parkour team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Traceurs, Vault Masters or Wall Runners, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Traceurs, Vault Masters or Wall Runners, so the name clearly belongs to parkour.
    • Say it out loud. A good parkour team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
    • A word from the sport itself. For parkour that means Traceurs, Vault Masters or Wall Runners, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
