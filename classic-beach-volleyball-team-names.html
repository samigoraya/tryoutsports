    What makes a good classic beach volleyball team name?

    Classic names lean on tradition, trades and hometown pride. They are short, timeless and look good stitched across a jersey. For beach volleyball, that means mixing classic words with the sport itself: Sand Diggers, Sun Spikers and Surf Setters all work well.

    • Pair your town with a trade or tradition, like Millers, Pioneers or Railmen.
    • Keep it to two words so it fits a jersey and reads clearly on a scoreboard.
    • Pick a name that will still sound good in ten years.
    • Mix in beach volleyball words like Sand Diggers, Sun Spikers or Surf Setters to tie the name to the sport.
