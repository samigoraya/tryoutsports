    What makes a good mythical powerlifting team name?

    Mythical names draw on dragons, phoenixes, titans and legends. They feel epic and make great logos. For powerlifting, that means mixing mythical words with the sport itself: Powerlifters, Squat Crew and Bench Press Crew all work well.

    • Pick a creature with a strong silhouette: Dragons, Griffins or Phoenixes.
    • Add a mythic word like Ancient, Immortal, Eternal or Legend.
    • Borrow from the myths of your own region for a local twist.
    • Mix in powerlifting words like Powerlifters, Squat Crew or Bench Press Crew to tie the name to the sport.

    Mythical words that fit powerlifting

    Mythical names mix a describing word with a powerlifting word. Try describing words like Legendary, Golden, Frost, Thunder, Fabled, Mystic, Crystal, Eternal, Emerald, Immortal, next to powerlifting words like Rack Pullers, Iron Crew, Squat Crew, Powerlifters, Plate Pushers, Total Crew, Deadlift Demons, Platform Pros. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
