    About free diving

    Freediving is diving on a single breath, with disciplines for depth, distance and static breath-hold. Safety divers and a support team make every record attempt possible, so clubs matter even in a solo sport. France, Italy, Russia, New Zealand, Japan and Croatia lead the sport.

    What makes a good free diving team name?

    Free diving is breath-hold diving without tanks, built on calm control and training. The best free diving team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Free Divers, Breath Holders or Depth Seekers, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Free Divers, Breath Holders or Depth Seekers, so the name clearly belongs to free diving.
    • Say it out loud. A good free diving team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A word from the sport itself. For free diving that means Free Divers, Breath Holders or Depth Seekers, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Alliance, Syndicate, Collective or Brigade. Try your town plus Crew for a free diving side.
