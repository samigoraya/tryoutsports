    About decathlon and heptathlon

    The decathlon for men and heptathlon for women spread sprints, jumps and throws over two days, with a points table deciding the winner. Combined-events athletes are the all-rounders of athletics and often train in tight-knit groups. France, Canada, Germany, Belgium, Great Britain and the United States are among the leaders.

    What makes a good decathlon and heptathlon team name?

    Decathlon and heptathlon are multi-event contests that crown the all-round athletes of track and field. The best decathlon and heptathlon team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Decathletes, Heptathletes or Ten Event Titans, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Decathletes, Heptathletes or Ten Event Titans, so the name clearly belongs to decathlon and heptathlon.
    • Say it out loud. A good decathlon and heptathlon team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers, Alliance, Athletic or Sporting. Columbus Crew and Philadelphia Union are built this way, and it works for any sport. Try your town plus Crew for a decathlon and heptathlon side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For decathlon and heptathlon that means Decathletes, Heptathletes or Ten Event Titans, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
