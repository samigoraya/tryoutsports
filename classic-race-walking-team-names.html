    What makes a good classic race walking team name?

    Classic names lean on tradition, trades and hometown pride. They are short, timeless and look good stitched across a jersey. For race walking, that means mixing classic words with the sport itself: Walkers, Heel Toe Heroes and Straight Knees all work well.

    • Pair your town with a trade or tradition, like Millers, Pioneers or Railmen.
    • Keep it to two words so it fits a jersey and reads clearly on a scoreboard.
    • Pick a name that will still sound good in ten years.
    • Mix in race walking words like Walkers, Heel Toe Heroes or Straight Knees to tie the name to the sport.

    Classic words that fit race walking

    Classic names mix a describing word with a race walking word. Try describing words like River, Mountain, Canyon, Liberty, Diamond, Summer, Silver, Copper, Old, Golden, next to race walking words like Gait Gurus, Stride Masters, Contact Keepers, Judges Eyes, Walkers, Pace Walkers, Straight Knees, Road Walkers, Fast Feet. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
