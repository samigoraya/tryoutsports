    What makes a good spooky road cycling team name?

    Spooky names use ghosts, graveyards and midnight. They are playful rather than scary, and great for Halloween leagues. For road cycling, that means mixing spooky words with the sport itself: Peloton, Breakaways and Climbers all work well.

    • Try ghosts, ghouls, bats and phantoms for a classic spooky feel.
    • Add time words like Midnight, Moonlit or Haunted.
    • Black, purple and orange give a Halloween look.
    • Mix in road cycling words like Peloton, Breakaways or Climbers to tie the name to the sport.

    Spooky words that fit road cycling

    Spooky names mix a describing word with a road cycling word. Try describing words like Shadow, Restless, Vanishing, Creepy, Dark, Eerie, Grim, Hollow, Foggy, Ghostly, next to road cycling words like Echelon Crew, Climbers, Gear Grinders, Sprinters, Peloton, Yellow Jerseys, Breakaways, Wheel Suckers, Domestiques, Pace Line. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
