    What makes a good mythical baseball team name?

    Mythical names draw on dragons, titans and spellcasters. They pair well with bold logos and give a team a story to tell.

    • Pick a creature with a strong silhouette: Dragons, Griffins, Phoenixes, Krakens.
    • Add an element for punch: Fire Dragons, Frost Giants, Thunder Gods.
    • Use myth roles such as Oracles, Argonauts or Valkyries for a less common choice.
    • Choose a creature that is easy to draw, since it will end up on your cap.
