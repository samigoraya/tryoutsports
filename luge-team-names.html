    About luge

    Luge is raced feet first on a flat sled, in singles and doubles, with steering done by shifting the shoulders and legs. The team relay sends singles and doubles sliders down in sequence, touching a pad to release the next. Germany, Austria, Italy, Latvia and Russia lead the sport.

    • Team size1 or 2 sliders, with team relays
    • Biggest eventsWinter Olympics, FIL World Luge Championships
    • Sport groupWinter Sports

    What makes a good luge team name?

    Luge is feet-first sled racing down an ice track, with riders lying on their backs. The best luge team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Lugers, Ice Sliders or Sled Riders, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Lugers, Ice Sliders or Sled Riders, so the name clearly belongs to luge.
    • Say it out loud. A good luge team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Alliance, Syndicate, Collective or Brigade. Try your town plus Crew for a luge side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A word from the sport itself. For luge that means Lugers, Ice Sliders or Sled Riders, which tells everyone what you play.
