    What makes a good cute road cycling team name?

    Cute names use baby animals, sweets and sunshine. They are warm, easy for kids to say and easy to draw. For road cycling, that means mixing cute words with the sport itself: Peloton, Breakaways and Climbers all work well.

    • Choose animals kids love: Bunnies, Ducklings, Pandas or Puppies.
    • Add a friendly word like Happy, Sunny, Fluffy or Tiny.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw so kids can doodle it.
    • Mix in road cycling words like Peloton, Breakaways or Climbers to tie the name to the sport.
