    What makes a good mythical track cycling team name?

    Mythical names draw on dragons, phoenixes, titans and legends. They feel epic and make great logos. For track cycling, that means mixing mythical words with the sport itself: Velodrome Crew, Sprinters and Pursuiters all work well.

    • Pick a creature with a strong silhouette: Dragons, Griffins or Phoenixes.
    • Add a mythic word like Ancient, Immortal, Eternal or Legend.
    • Borrow from the myths of your own region for a local twist.
    • Mix in track cycling words like Velodrome Crew, Sprinters or Pursuiters to tie the name to the sport.

    Mythical words that fit track cycling

    Mythical names mix a describing word with a track cycling word. Try describing words like Shadow, Storm, Fabled, Emerald, Sacred, Ancient, Enchanted, Arcane, Moonlit, Flame, next to track cycling words like Track Burners, Pedal Power, Pursuiters, Sprinters, Velodrome Crew, Derny Dudes, Banking Bruisers, Madison Crew, Keirin Kings. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
