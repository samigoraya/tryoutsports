    What makes a good spooky baseball team name?

    Spooky names suit Halloween tournaments and night leagues, and they look great in black and orange.

    • Use classic monsters: Vampires, Werewolves, Mummies, Banshees.
    • Add night words: Midnight, Moonlit, Haunted, Shadow.
    • Try spooky objects such as Lanterns, Coffins or Cobwebs for something less expected.
    • Black and orange make the strongest uniform pairing.

    Questions people ask

    What is a good spooky baseball team name?

    Creatures and night words work best, for example Phantoms, Night Stalkers, Bone Rattlers or Full Moons.

    Are spooky names suitable for kids teams?

    Many are. Pumpkins, Bats and Black Cats feel playful rather than scary, so pick the gentler ones for younger players.

    Which tournaments suit a spooky team name?

    Halloween weekends and night leagues are the obvious fit, though a spooky name works all season if the logo is strong.
