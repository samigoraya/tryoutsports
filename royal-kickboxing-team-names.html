    What makes a good royal kickboxing team name?

    Royal names borrow crowns, castles and noble titles. They sound proud and look sharp on gold-trimmed kit. For kickboxing, that means mixing royal words with the sport itself: Low Kickers, Roundhouse Kings and Shin Crushers all work well.

    • Use titles like Kings, Queens, Barons, Knights or Monarchs.
    • Add crowns, castles and thrones for a regal image.
    • Gold, purple and deep red are the classic royal colors.
    • Mix in kickboxing words like Low Kickers, Roundhouse Kings or Shin Crushers to tie the name to the sport.

    Royal words that fit kickboxing

    Royal names mix a describing word with a kickboxing word. Try describing words like Supreme, Ivory, Grand, Velvet, Noble, Crowned, Honored, Silver, Gallant, Gilded, next to kickboxing words like Body Kickers, Ring Warriors, Shin Crushers, Heavy Bags, Counter Strikers, Spin Kickers, Combo Throwers, Low Kickers, Knockout Kings, Roundhouse Kings. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
