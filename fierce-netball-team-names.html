    What makes a good fierce netball team name?

    Fierce names borrow from predators, storms and heavy metal. They are short, hard-sounding and built to be chanted. For netball, that means mixing fierce words with the sport itself: Goal Shooters, Goal Attacks and Wing Attacks all work well.

    • Pick predators with a strong sound: Vipers, Wolves, Raptors or Sharks.
    • Add weather for power: Thunder, Cyclone, Avalanche or Storm.
    • Use one hard word when you want an intimidating name that is easy to chant.
    • Mix in netball words like Goal Shooters, Goal Attacks or Wing Attacks to tie the name to the sport.

    Fierce words that fit netball

    Fierce names mix a describing word with a netball word. Try describing words like Shadow, Raging, Wild, Roaring, Desert, Crimson, Lightning, Storm, Night, Steel, next to netball words like Chest Passes, Rapid Fire, Shoulder Passes, Pivots, Intercepts, Goal Shooters, Goal Attacks, Goal Keepers, Circle Edge, Rebounders. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
