    What makes a good fierce BASE jumping team name?

    Fierce names borrow from predators, storms and heavy metal. They are short, hard-sounding and built to be chanted. For BASE jumping, that means mixing fierce words with the sport itself: Base Jumpers, Cliff Leapers and Wingsuit Crew all work well.

    • Pick predators with a strong sound: Vipers, Wolves, Raptors or Sharks.
    • Add weather for power: Thunder, Cyclone, Avalanche or Storm.
    • Use one hard word when you want an intimidating name that is easy to chant.
    • Mix in BASE jumping words like Base Jumpers, Cliff Leapers or Wingsuit Crew to tie the name to the sport.
