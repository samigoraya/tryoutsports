    About taekwondo

    Taekwondo is the Korean kicking art, scored electronically on a mat over three rounds, with head kicks worth the most. Poomsae, the forms competition, is also judged in teams. South Korea, Iran, Turkey, Russia, Spain and China are the main powers.

    What makes a good taekwondo team name?

    Taekwondo is a Korean martial art known for fast, high kicks and sparring with electronic scoring. The best taekwondo team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Kickers, Spin Kickers or Roundhouses, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Kickers, Spin Kickers or Roundhouses, so the name clearly belongs to taekwondo.
    • Say it out loud. A good taekwondo team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A word from the sport itself. For taekwondo that means Kickers, Spin Kickers or Roundhouses, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Alliance, Syndicate, Collective or Brigade. Try your town plus Crew for a taekwondo side.
