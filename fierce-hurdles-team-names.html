    What makes a good fierce hurdles team name?

    Fierce names borrow from predators, storms and heavy metal. They are short, hard-sounding and built to be chanted. For hurdles, that means mixing fierce words with the sport itself: Hurdlers, Lead Legs and Trail Legs all work well.

    • Pick predators with a strong sound: Vipers, Wolves, Raptors or Sharks.
    • Add weather for power: Thunder, Cyclone, Avalanche or Storm.
    • Use one hard word when you want an intimidating name that is easy to chant.
    • Mix in hurdles words like Hurdlers, Lead Legs or Trail Legs to tie the name to the sport.

    Fierce words that fit hurdles

    Fierce names mix a describing word with a hurdles word. Try describing words like Thunder, Iron, Raging, Storm, Crimson, Desert, Fire, Fighting, Black, Roaring, next to hurdles words like Trail Legs, Clearing Kings, Hurdlers, Steeplechasers, Hurdle Hoppers, Flat Runners, Lead Legs, Rhythm Runners, High Steppers, Jump Masters. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
