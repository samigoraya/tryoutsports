    About basketball

    Basketball is played on a hard court by two teams of five who dribble, pass and shoot at a hoop ten feet up.     What makes a good basketball team name?

    Basketball is a fast indoor game of dribbling, passing and shooting at raised hoops. The best basketball team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Dunkers, Rebounders or Shooters, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Dunkers, Rebounders or Shooters, so the name clearly belongs to basketball.
    • Say it out loud. A good basketball team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Match the tone to the team: fierce for competitive squads, funny or cute for social and youth teams.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers, Alliance, Athletic or Sporting. Columbus Crew and Philadelphia Union are built this way, and it works for any sport. Try your town plus Crew for a basketball side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For basketball that means Dunkers, Rebounders or Shooters, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
