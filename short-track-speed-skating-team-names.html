    About short track speed skating

    Short track races four to six skaters at once around a 111m oval inside a hockey rink, with contact, passing and crashes all part of it. The relay is a spectacular team event with hand pushes to launch the next skater. South Korea, China, the Netherlands, Canada, Italy and Hungary lead the sport.

    • Team size1 skater, with relays of 4 or 5
    • Biggest eventsWinter Olympics, ISU World Short Track Championships
    • Sport groupWinter Sports

    What makes a good short track speed skating team name?

    Short track speed skating is a tight, tactical pack race on a small oval. The best short track speed skating team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Short Trackers, Pack Racers or Corner Cutters, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Short Trackers, Pack Racers or Corner Cutters, so the name clearly belongs to short track speed skating.
    • Say it out loud. A good short track speed skating team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Match the tone to the team: fierce for competitive squads, funny or cute for social and youth teams.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers, Alliance, Athletic or Sporting. Columbus Crew and Philadelphia Union are built this way, and it works for any sport. Try your town plus Crew for a short track speed skating side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For short track speed skating that means Short Trackers, Pack Racers or Corner Cutters, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
