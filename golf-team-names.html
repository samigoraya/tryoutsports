    About golf

    Golf is played over 18 holes, counting strokes, with the lowest total winning. Team golf is a big part of the calendar: the Ryder Cup, Presidents Cup and club matchplay all put players under one name. The United States, Europe, South Korea, Japan and Australia supply most of the world's best.

    What makes a good golf team name?

    Golf is a club-and-ball game played over 18 holes, with team formats for leagues, scrambles and charity events. The best golf team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Birdies, Eagles or Aces, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Birdies, Eagles or Aces, so the name clearly belongs to golf.
    • Say it out loud. A good golf team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.
    • Match the tone to the team: fierce for competitive squads, funny or cute for social and youth teams.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A word from the sport itself. For golf that means Birdies, Eagles or Aces, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
