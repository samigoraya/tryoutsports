    About roller hockey

    Roller hockey covers two codes: rink hockey on quad skates with a curved stick and a ball, and inline hockey on inline skates with a puck. Both are fast, end-to-end games on a hard surface. Portugal, Spain, Argentina and Italy lead rink hockey, while the United States, Canada and the Czech Republic are strong at inline.

    • Team size5 a side in rink hockey, 5 in inline hockey
    • Biggest eventsRink Hockey World Cup, World Skate Games

    What makes a good roller hockey team name?

    Roller hockey is played on wheels, either as quad hockey or inline hockey, with fast end-to-end play. The best roller hockey team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Rollers, Wheelers or Skaters, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Rollers, Wheelers or Skaters, so the name clearly belongs to roller hockey.
    • Say it out loud. A good roller hockey team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Alliance, Syndicate, Collective or Brigade. Try your town plus Crew for a roller hockey side.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For roller hockey that means Rollers, Wheelers or Skaters, which tells everyone what you play.
