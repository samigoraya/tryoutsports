    What makes a good classic fencing team name?

    Classic names lean on tradition, trades and hometown pride. They are short, timeless and look good stitched across a jersey. For fencing, that means mixing classic words with the sport itself: Foils, Epees and Sabres all work well.

    • Pair your town with a trade or tradition, like Millers, Pioneers or Railmen.
    • Keep it to two words so it fits a jersey and reads clearly on a scoreboard.
    • Pick a name that will still sound good in ten years.
    • Mix in fencing words like Foils, Epees or Sabres to tie the name to the sport.

    Classic words that fit fencing

    Classic names mix a describing word with a fencing word. Try describing words like Old, Grand, Valley, Royal, Golden, River, Iron, Harbor, Prairie, Copper, next to fencing words like Sabres, Foils, Duellists, En Garde, Piste Kings, Lunges, Fleches, Ripostes, Point Scorers, Epees. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
