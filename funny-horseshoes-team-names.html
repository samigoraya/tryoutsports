    What makes a good funny horseshoes team name?

    The best funny names turn a word from the sport into a joke: a pun, an odd animal or a snack. If it makes your own team laugh, it works. For horseshoes, that means mixing funny words with the sport itself: Ringers, Pitchers and Stake Hitters all work well.

    • Twist a word from your sport into a pun.
    • Add food for an easy laugh: Waffles, Pickles or Nachos.
    • Put a goofy adjective on the front: Sleepy, Soggy or Wobbly.
    • Mix in horseshoes words like Ringers, Pitchers or Stake Hitters to tie the name to the sport.

    Funny words that fit horseshoes

    Funny names mix a describing word with a horseshoes word. Try describing words like Confused, Soggy, Wobbly, Wandering, Slightly, Mighty, Juggling, Fancy, Grumpy, Lost, next to horseshoes words like Iron Throwers, Stake Hitters, Leaners, Ringers, Clang Crew, Flip Pitchers, Lucky Shoes, Backyard Champs, Shoe Throwers, Pitchers. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
