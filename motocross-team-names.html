    About motocross

    Motocross races on outdoor dirt circuits with jumps, ruts and berms, over two timed motos. The Motocross of Nations puts three riders together for one country, which is the sport's biggest team day. The Netherlands, France, Belgium, the United States, Italy and Australia are the strongest nations.

    What makes a good motocross team name?

    Motocross is off-road motorcycle racing on dirt tracks with jumps, berms and ruts. The best motocross team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Moto Masters, Whoop Riders or Berm Blasters, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Moto Masters, Whoop Riders or Berm Blasters, so the name clearly belongs to motocross.
    • Say it out loud. A good motocross team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers, Alliance, Athletic or Sporting. Columbus Crew and Philadelphia Union are built this way, and it works for any sport. Try your town plus Crew for a motocross side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For motocross that means Moto Masters, Whoop Riders or Berm Blasters, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
