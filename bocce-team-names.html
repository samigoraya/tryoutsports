    About bocce

    Bocce rolls heavy balls toward a smaller target ball called the pallino, scoring for the closest balls at the end of each frame. It is played on courts in clubs and parks, especially in Italy and Italian communities worldwide. Teams of two or four make it one of the most sociable precision sports.

    • Team sizeTeams of 1, 2 or 4
    • Biggest eventsWorld Bocce Championships, Special Olympics bocce
    • Sport groupTarget & Precision Sports

    What makes a good bocce team name?

    Bocce is an Italian lawn bowling game, with teams rolling balls to land close to the pallino. The best bocce team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Pallino Pushers, Bocce Bashers or Roll Masters, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Pallino Pushers, Bocce Bashers or Roll Masters, so the name clearly belongs to bocce.
    • Say it out loud. A good bocce team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Match the tone to the team: fierce for competitive squads, funny or cute for social and youth teams.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A word from the sport itself. For bocce that means Pallino Pushers, Bocce Bashers or Roll Masters, which tells everyone what you play.
    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Alliance, Syndicate, Collective or Brigade. Try your town plus Crew for a bocce side.
