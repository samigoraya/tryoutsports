    About volleyball

    Volleyball is a net game where each side gets three touches to dig, set and spike the ball back. Sets are played to 25 points, and the whole team rotates position after each won serve. It is a staple of school, college and company leagues all over the world.

    What makes a good volleyball team name?

    Volleyball is a net game of serves, sets and spikes, played indoors or outdoors. The best volleyball team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Spikers, Setters or Liberos, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Spikers, Setters or Liberos, so the name clearly belongs to volleyball.
    • Say it out loud. A good volleyball team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers, Alliance, Athletic or Sporting. Columbus Crew and Philadelphia Union are built this way, and it works for any sport. Try your town plus Crew for a volleyball side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For volleyball that means Spikers, Setters or Liberos, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
