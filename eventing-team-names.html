    About eventing

    Eventing is the triathlon of equestrian sport, combining dressage, cross-country and show jumping over up to three days. Team events use three riders with no drop score at the Olympics, so every round counts. Great Britain, Germany, Australia, New Zealand, France and the United States lead the sport.

    • Team size1 rider and horse, with teams of 3 or 4
    • Biggest eventsOlympic Games, Badminton and Burghley Horse Trials
    • Sport groupEquestrian Sports

    What makes a good eventing team name?

    Eventing combines dressage, cross-country and show jumping into one test. The best eventing team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Eventers, Triple Challenge or Cross Country Crew, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Eventers, Triple Challenge or Cross Country Crew, so the name clearly belongs to eventing.
    • Say it out loud. A good eventing team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers, Alliance, Athletic or Sporting. Columbus Crew and Philadelphia Union are built this way, and it works for any sport. Try your town plus Crew for a eventing side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For eventing that means Eventers, Triple Challenge or Cross Country Crew, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
