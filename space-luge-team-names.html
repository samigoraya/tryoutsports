    What makes a good space luge team name?

    Space names borrow rockets, planets and galaxies. They sound fast and futuristic and look great with metallic colors. For luge, that means mixing space words with the sport itself: Lugers, Ice Sliders and Sled Riders all work well.

    • Use planets and objects: Comets, Meteors, Rockets or Nebulas.
    • Add a futuristic word like Cosmic, Stellar, Orbital or Quantum.
    • Silver, navy and electric blue match the theme.
    • Mix in luge words like Lugers, Ice Sliders or Sled Riders to tie the name to the sport.

    Space words that fit luge

    Space names mix a describing word with a luge word. Try describing words like Electric, Quantum, Cosmic, Lunar, Atomic, Orbital, Astro, Hyper, Turbo, Sonic, next to luge words like Track Racers, Lugers, Speed Sliders, Steel Runners, Sled Riders, Ice Sliders, Frozen Flyers, Ice Slicers, Shoulder Steerers. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
