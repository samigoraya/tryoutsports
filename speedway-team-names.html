    About speedway

    Speedway races four riders at a time on 500cc bikes with no brakes, sliding broadside around a short oval for four laps. League speedway is a genuine team sport, with clubs of seven riders scoring heat points. Poland, Great Britain, Sweden, Denmark and Australia have the strongest leagues.

    • Team size1 rider, with league teams of 7
    • Biggest eventsSpeedway Grand Prix, Speedway World Cup, Ekstraliga
    • Sport groupMotorsports

    What makes a good speedway team name?

    Speedway is motorcycle racing on short dirt ovals, with bikes that have no brakes. The best speedway team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Speedway Riders, Cinder Sliders or Dirt Oval Crew, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Speedway Riders, Cinder Sliders or Dirt Oval Crew, so the name clearly belongs to speedway.
    • Say it out loud. A good speedway team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.
    • Match the tone to the team: fierce for competitive squads, funny or cute for social and youth teams.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers, Alliance, Athletic or Sporting. Columbus Crew and Philadelphia Union are built this way, and it works for any sport. Try your town plus Crew for a speedway side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For speedway that means Speedway Riders, Cinder Sliders or Dirt Oval Crew, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
