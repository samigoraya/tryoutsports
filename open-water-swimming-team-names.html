    About open water swimming

    Open water swimming races in lakes, rivers and seas over 5km, 10km and 25km, with the 10km on the Olympic programme. The mixed relay sends four swimmers out in turn. Germany, Hungary, Italy, France, the Netherlands and Australia are the strongest nations.

    What makes a good open water swimming team name?

    Open water swimming takes place in lakes, rivers and the sea, often over long distances. The best open water swimming team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Open Water Swimmers, Wave Wrestlers or Buoy Turners, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Open Water Swimmers, Wave Wrestlers or Buoy Turners, so the name clearly belongs to open water swimming.
    • Say it out loud. A good open water swimming team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
    • A word from the sport itself. For open water swimming that means Open Water Swimmers, Wave Wrestlers or Buoy Turners, which tells everyone what you play.
