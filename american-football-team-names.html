    About American football

    American football is played in short bursts called downs, with an offense getting four attempts to move the ball ten yards. Scoring comes from touchdowns, field goals and conversions. Because squads are big and split into units, team names here have to work for dozens of players and a loud stadium crowd.

    What makes a good American football team name?

    American football is a tackle sport of downs, drives and set plays played on a 100-yard field. The best American football team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Gridiron Kings, Linebackers or Quarterbacks, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Gridiron Kings, Linebackers or Quarterbacks, so the name clearly belongs to American football.
    • Say it out loud. A good American football team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
    • A word from the sport itself. For American football that means Gridiron Kings, Linebackers or Quarterbacks, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
