    What makes a good cute polo team name?

    Cute names use baby animals, sweets and sunshine. They are warm, easy for kids to say and easy to draw. For polo, that means mixing cute words with the sport itself: Mallet Masters, Chukker Champs and Pony Players all work well.

    • Choose animals kids love: Bunnies, Ducklings, Pandas or Puppies.
    • Add a friendly word like Happy, Sunny, Fluffy or Tiny.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw so kids can doodle it.
    • Mix in polo words like Mallet Masters, Chukker Champs or Pony Players to tie the name to the sport.

    Cute words that fit polo

    Cute names mix a describing word with a polo word. Try describing words like Peppy, Bright, Lucky, Zippy, Cozy, Happy, Sunny, Little, Sparkly, Cheery, next to polo words like Neck Shots, Pony Players, Polo Ponies, Ride Offs, Back Hands, Field Flyers, Chukker Champs, Goal Hitters, Mallet Masters. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
