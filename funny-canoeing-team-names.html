    What makes a good funny canoeing team name?

    The best funny names turn a word from the sport into a joke: a pun, an odd animal or a snack. If it makes your own team laugh, it works. For canoeing, that means mixing funny words with the sport itself: Canoeists, Paddle Pushers and Portage Pros all work well.

    • Twist a word from your sport into a pun.
    • Add food for an easy laugh: Waffles, Pickles or Nachos.
    • Put a goofy adjective on the front: Sleepy, Soggy or Wobbly.
    • Mix in canoeing words like Canoeists, Paddle Pushers or Portage Pros to tie the name to the sport.

    Funny words that fit canoeing

    Funny names mix a describing word with a canoeing word. Try describing words like Fearless, Wobbly, Fancy, Soggy, Slightly, Hungry, Clumsy, Grumpy, Sneezing, Mighty, next to canoeing words like Birchbark Crew, Paddle Pushers, Gate Dodgers, Sprint Canoes, Slalom Runners, River Runners, Canoeists, Portage Pros, Blade Pullers, Wilderness Paddlers. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
