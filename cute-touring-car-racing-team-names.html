    What makes a good cute touring car racing team name?

    Cute names use baby animals, sweets and sunshine. They are warm, easy for kids to say and easy to draw. For touring car racing, that means mixing cute words with the sport itself: Touring Cars, Door To Door and Paint Swappers all work well.

    • Choose animals kids love: Bunnies, Ducklings, Pandas or Puppies.
    • Add a friendly word like Happy, Sunny, Fluffy or Tiny.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw so kids can doodle it.
    • Mix in touring car racing words like Touring Cars, Door To Door or Paint Swappers to tie the name to the sport.

    Cute words that fit touring car racing

    Cute names mix a describing word with a touring car racing word. Try describing words like Little, Peppy, Sweet, Happy, Merry, Snuggly, Giggly, Zippy, Bouncy, Bright, next to touring car racing words like Paint Swappers, Touring Cars, Podium Crew, Chicane Crew, Hot Hatches, Grid Crew, Sprint Racers, Saloon Racers. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
