    What makes a good classic CrossFit team name?

    Classic names lean on tradition, trades and hometown pride. They are short, timeless and look good stitched across a jersey. For CrossFit, that means mixing classic words with the sport itself: WOD Warriors, Box Crew and AMRAP Crew all work well.

    • Pair your town with a trade or tradition, like Millers, Pioneers or Railmen.
    • Keep it to two words so it fits a jersey and reads clearly on a scoreboard.
    • Pick a name that will still sound good in ten years.
    • Mix in CrossFit words like WOD Warriors, Box Crew or AMRAP Crew to tie the name to the sport.

    Classic words that fit CrossFit

    Classic names mix a describing word with a CrossFit word. Try describing words like Silver, Harbor, Copper, Dusty, Union, Summer, Old, Liberty, Grand, Coastal, next to CrossFit words like Metcon Mob, Burpee Brigade, Rope Climbers, Clean Machines, AMRAP Crew, Kettlebell Crew, Rx Crew, Box Crew, WOD Warriors. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
