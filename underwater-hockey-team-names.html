    About underwater hockey

    Underwater hockey, also called octopush, is played on the bottom of a swimming pool with short sticks, fins, masks and snorkels. Players surface to breathe while teammates keep the puck moving. Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, France and Great Britain are the leading nations.

    What makes a good underwater hockey team name?

    Underwater hockey, or octopush, is played on the bottom of a pool with short sticks, fins and snorkels. The best underwater hockey team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Octopushers, Puck Pushers or Snorkelers, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Octopushers, Puck Pushers or Snorkelers, so the name clearly belongs to underwater hockey.
    • Say it out loud. A good underwater hockey team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.
    • Match the tone to the team: fierce for competitive squads, funny or cute for social and youth teams.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Alliance, Syndicate, Collective or Brigade. Try your town plus Crew for a underwater hockey side.
    • A word from the sport itself. For underwater hockey that means Octopushers, Puck Pushers or Snorkelers, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
