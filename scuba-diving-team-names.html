    About scuba diving

    Scuba diving is done with a tank and regulator, almost always in buddy pairs, and its sporting side includes underwater orienteering, photography and finswimming. Dive clubs organise trips, training and competitions. It is practised everywhere there is water, with Egypt, Indonesia, Australia and Mexico among the famous destinations.

    What makes a good scuba diving team name?

    Scuba diving lets divers explore reefs, wrecks and caves with breathing equipment. The best scuba diving team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Scuba Squad, Reef Explorers or Wreck Divers, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Scuba Squad, Reef Explorers or Wreck Divers, so the name clearly belongs to scuba diving.
    • Say it out loud. A good scuba diving team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers, Alliance, Athletic or Sporting. Columbus Crew and Philadelphia Union are built this way, and it works for any sport. Try your town plus Crew for a scuba diving side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For scuba diving that means Scuba Squad, Reef Explorers or Wreck Divers, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
