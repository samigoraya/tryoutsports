    What makes a good spooky softball team name?

    Spooky names use ghosts, graveyards and midnight. They are playful rather than scary, and great for Halloween leagues. For softball, that means mixing spooky words with the sport itself: Windmills, Riseballs and Dropballs all work well.

    • Try ghosts, ghouls, bats and phantoms for a classic spooky feel.
    • Add time words like Midnight, Moonlit or Haunted.
    • Black, purple and orange give a Halloween look.
    • Mix in softball words like Windmills, Riseballs or Dropballs to tie the name to the sport.

    Spooky words that fit softball

    Spooky names mix a describing word with a softball word. Try describing words like Grim, Gloomy, Vanishing, Restless, Wicked, Creepy, Dark, Hollow, Screaming, Shadow, next to softball words like Dropballs, Sliders, Basepaths, Outfielders, Triple Plays, Riseballs, Grand Slams, Fastpitchers, Slappers, Pitchers. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
