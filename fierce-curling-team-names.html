    What makes a good fierce curling team name?

    Fierce names borrow from predators, storms and heavy metal. They are short, hard-sounding and built to be chanted. For curling, that means mixing fierce words with the sport itself: Curlers, Sweepers and Skips all work well.

    • Pick predators with a strong sound: Vipers, Wolves, Raptors or Sharks.
    • Add weather for power: Thunder, Cyclone, Avalanche or Storm.
    • Use one hard word when you want an intimidating name that is easy to chant.
    • Mix in curling words like Curlers, Sweepers or Skips to tie the name to the sport.

    Fierce words that fit curling

    Fierce names mix a describing word with a curling word. Try describing words like Savage, Wild, Rogue, Lightning, Desert, Fighting, Black, Raging, Steel, Storm, next to curling words like Hog Liners, Sweepers, House Holders, Button Biters, Stone Throwers, Brush Brigade, Draw Masters, Hammer Holders, Rock Rollers, Curlers. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
