    About racquetball

    Racquetball is played on an enclosed court with a short, strung racquet and a lively ball, with the ceiling in play as well as the walls. It is quick to learn and forgiving on technique, which makes it popular in gyms and clubs. The United States, Mexico, Bolivia and Costa Rica are the main competitive nations.

    What makes a good racquetball team name?

    Racquetball is a fast indoor court game played with a hollow rubber ball and short strung racquets. The best racquetball team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Ceiling Shots, Pinch Shots or Kill Shots, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Ceiling Shots, Pinch Shots or Kill Shots, so the name clearly belongs to racquetball.
    • Say it out loud. A good racquetball team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers, Alliance, Athletic or Sporting. Columbus Crew and Philadelphia Union are built this way, and it works for any sport. Try your town plus Crew for a racquetball side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For racquetball that means Ceiling Shots, Pinch Shots or Kill Shots, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
