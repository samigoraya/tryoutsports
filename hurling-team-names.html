    About hurling

    Hurling is one of the fastest field sports in the world, an ancient Irish game played with a wooden stick called a hurley and a small ball called a sliotar. Players can strike it in the air or along the ground and carry it on the stick. Like Gaelic football, it is amateur, club-based and fiercely local.

    • Team size15 a side, with a hurley and a sliotar
    • Biggest eventsAll-Ireland Senior Hurling Championship

    What makes a good hurling team name?

    Hurling is an ancient Irish stick-and-ball sport, played at great speed with a hurley and a sliotar. The best hurling team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Hurlers, Sliotars or Hurleys, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Hurlers, Sliotars or Hurleys, so the name clearly belongs to hurling.
    • Say it out loud. A good hurling team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Match the tone to the team: fierce for competitive squads, funny or cute for social and youth teams.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
    • A word from the sport itself. For hurling that means Hurlers, Sliotars or Hurleys, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
