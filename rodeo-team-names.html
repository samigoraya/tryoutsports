    About rodeo

    Rodeo runs timed events such as barrel racing and roping and judged events such as bull and bronc riding. Team roping is the one true team event, pairing a header and a heeler. The United States, Canada, Brazil, Mexico and Australia have the biggest rodeo circuits.

    • Team size1 competitor per event, with team roping of 2
    • Biggest eventsNational Finals Rodeo, Calgary Stampede
    • Sport groupEquestrian Sports

    What makes a good rodeo team name?

    Rodeo is a western contest of bronc riding, bull riding, roping and steer events. The best rodeo team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Bronc Busters, Bull Riders or Ropers, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Bronc Busters, Bull Riders or Ropers, so the name clearly belongs to rodeo.
    • Say it out loud. A good rodeo team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
    • A word from the sport itself. For rodeo that means Bronc Busters, Bull Riders or Ropers, which tells everyone what you play.
