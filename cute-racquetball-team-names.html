    What makes a good cute racquetball team name?

    Cute names use baby animals, sweets and sunshine. They are warm, easy for kids to say and easy to draw. For racquetball, that means mixing cute words with the sport itself: Ceiling Shots, Pinch Shots and Kill Shots all work well.

    • Choose animals kids love: Bunnies, Ducklings, Pandas or Puppies.
    • Add a friendly word like Happy, Sunny, Fluffy or Tiny.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw so kids can doodle it.
    • Mix in racquetball words like Ceiling Shots, Pinch Shots or Kill Shots to tie the name to the sport.

    Cute words that fit racquetball

    Cute names mix a describing word with a racquetball word. Try describing words like Cheery, Peppy, Sweet, Tiny, Merry, Cozy, Sunny, Bouncy, Little, Lucky, next to racquetball words like Z Serves, Ceiling Shots, Passing Shots, Wall Bangers, Pinch Shots, Court Kings, Rollouts, Drive Serves, Rally Crushers, Kill Shots. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
