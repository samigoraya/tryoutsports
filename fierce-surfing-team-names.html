    What makes a good fierce surfing team name?

    Fierce names borrow from predators, storms and heavy metal. They are short, hard-sounding and built to be chanted. For surfing, that means mixing fierce words with the sport itself: Wave Riders, Barrel Hunters and Swell Seekers all work well.

    • Pick predators with a strong sound: Vipers, Wolves, Raptors or Sharks.
    • Add weather for power: Thunder, Cyclone, Avalanche or Storm.
    • Use one hard word when you want an intimidating name that is easy to chant.
    • Mix in surfing words like Wave Riders, Barrel Hunters or Swell Seekers to tie the name to the sport.

    Fierce words that fit surfing

    Fierce names mix a describing word with a surfing word. Try describing words like Night, Fighting, Shadow, Wild, Lightning, Desert, Iron, Midnight, Thunder, Roaring, next to surfing words like Rip Curlers, Swell Seekers, Pipeline Pros, Tube Riders, Curl Chasers, Longboarders, Board Slayers, Tide Turners, Barrel Hunters, Shredders. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
