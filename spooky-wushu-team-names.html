    What makes a good spooky wushu team name?

    Spooky names use ghosts, graveyards and midnight. They are playful rather than scary, and great for Halloween leagues. For wushu, that means mixing spooky words with the sport itself: Taolu Stars, Sanda Fighters and Form Masters all work well.
