    About sumo

    Sumo is Japan's traditional wrestling, where the aim is to force the opponent out of a straw ring or onto the ground, usually in seconds. Wrestlers live and train in a stable, and the stable is the team. Japan and Mongolia supply most of the top ranks today.

    What makes a good sumo team name?

    Sumo is a traditional Japanese wrestling sport where rikishi try to force each other out of the ring. The best sumo team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Rikishi, Yokozunas or Ring Pushers, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Rikishi, Yokozunas or Ring Pushers, so the name clearly belongs to sumo.
    • Say it out loud. A good sumo team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.
    • Match the tone to the team: fierce for competitive squads, funny or cute for social and youth teams.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A word from the sport itself. For sumo that means Rikishi, Yokozunas or Ring Pushers, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
