    About bungee jumping

    Bungee jumping drops a jumper on an elastic cord from a bridge, crane or platform, with the crew calculating cord length for each person's weight. There is no world championship, so the team is the operating crew or the club that organises jumps. New Zealand is where commercial bungee started, and sites now operate worldwide.

    • Team size1 jumper, with operator and crew teams
    • Biggest eventsRecreational and record jumps rather than a championship

    What makes a good bungee jumping team name?

    Bungee jumping is a leap from a height, held by an elastic cord. The best bungee jumping team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Bungee Crew, Free Fall Crew or Leap Takers, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Bungee Crew, Free Fall Crew or Leap Takers, so the name clearly belongs to bungee jumping.
    • Say it out loud. A good bungee jumping team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers, Alliance, Athletic or Sporting. Columbus Crew and Philadelphia Union are built this way, and it works for any sport. Try your town plus Crew for a bungee jumping side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For bungee jumping that means Bungee Crew, Free Fall Crew or Leap Takers, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
