    About ice climbing

    Ice climbing races up frozen waterfalls and artificial towers with axes and crampons, scored on speed or on how far you get in a lead route. National teams travel the World Cup circuit together. Russia, South Korea, France, Switzerland and Canada are among the strongest.

    What makes a good ice climbing team name?

    Ice climbing scales frozen waterfalls and glaciers using axes and crampons. The best ice climbing team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Ice Climbers, Axe Swingers or Crampon Crew, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Ice Climbers, Axe Swingers or Crampon Crew, so the name clearly belongs to ice climbing.
    • Say it out loud. A good ice climbing team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A word from the sport itself. For ice climbing that means Ice Climbers, Axe Swingers or Crampon Crew, which tells everyone what you play.
