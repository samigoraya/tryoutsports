    What makes a good spooky rafting team name?

    Spooky names use ghosts, graveyards and midnight. They are playful rather than scary, and great for Halloween leagues. For rafting, that means mixing spooky words with the sport itself: Rafters, Rapid Runners and Whitewater Warriors all work well.

    • Try ghosts, ghouls, bats and phantoms for a classic spooky feel.
    • Add time words like Midnight, Moonlit or Haunted.
    • Black, purple and orange give a Halloween look.
    • Mix in rafting words like Rafters, Rapid Runners or Whitewater Warriors to tie the name to the sport.

    Spooky words that fit rafting

    Spooky names mix a describing word with a rafting word. Try describing words like Ghostly, Grim, Dark, Foggy, Screaming, Cursed, Howling, Moonlit, Haunted, Midnight, next to rafting words like Rafters, Rapid Runners, Canyon Crew, Whitewater Warriors, Paddle Crew, Wave Crashers, River Guides, Raft Riders, Eddy Hunters, Rapids Rats. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
