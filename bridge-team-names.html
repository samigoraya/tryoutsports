    About bridge (card game)

    Contract bridge is played in partnerships, with four making a team in the standard teams-of-four format that decides world titles. Bidding, declarer play and defence all rely on trust between partners. Italy, the United States, Poland, Norway, the Netherlands and Sweden are among the strongest nations.

    • Team sizePartnerships of 2, teams of 4 to 6
    • Biggest eventsBermuda Bowl, World Bridge Championships

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A word from the sport itself. For bridge (card game) that means Declarers, Slam Seekers or Trump Crew, which tells everyone what you play.
