    What makes a good royal netball team name?

    Royal names borrow crowns, castles and noble titles. They sound proud and look sharp on gold-trimmed kit. For netball, that means mixing royal words with the sport itself: Goal Shooters, Goal Attacks and Wing Attacks all work well.

    • Use titles like Kings, Queens, Barons, Knights or Monarchs.
    • Add crowns, castles and thrones for a regal image.
    • Gold, purple and deep red are the classic royal colors.
    • Mix in netball words like Goal Shooters, Goal Attacks or Wing Attacks to tie the name to the sport.
