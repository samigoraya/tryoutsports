    About curling

    Curling slides stones down a sheet of ice toward a target, with two teammates sweeping to steer them, over eight or ten ends. It is one of the most tactical team sports in the Games, and rinks are often named after their skip. Canada, Sweden, Scotland, Switzerland, Norway and the United States are the strongest.

    • Team size4 a side, with mixed doubles of 2
    • Biggest eventsWinter Olympics, World Curling Championships, Scotties and Brier
    • Sport groupWinter Sports

    What makes a good curling team name?

    Curling is a sliding stone game on ice, where sweeping and strategy decide the end. The best curling team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Curlers, Sweepers or Skips, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Curlers, Sweepers or Skips, so the name clearly belongs to curling.
    • Say it out loud. A good curling team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Match the tone to the team: fierce for competitive squads, funny or cute for social and youth teams.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
    • A word from the sport itself. For curling that means Curlers, Sweepers or Skips, which tells everyone what you play.
