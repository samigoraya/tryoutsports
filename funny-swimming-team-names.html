    What makes a good funny swimming team name?

    The best funny names turn a word from the sport into a joke: a pun, an odd animal or a snack. If it makes your own team laugh, it works. For swimming, that means mixing funny words with the sport itself: Freestylers, Butterflies and Backstrokers all work well.

    • Twist a word from your sport into a pun.
    • Add food for an easy laugh: Waffles, Pickles or Nachos.
    • Put a goofy adjective on the front: Sleepy, Soggy or Wobbly.
    • Mix in swimming words like Freestylers, Butterflies or Backstrokers to tie the name to the sport.

    Funny words that fit swimming

    Funny names mix a describing word with a swimming word. Try describing words like Clumsy, Sleepy, Slightly, Juggling, Loud, Hungry, Mighty, Fancy, Confused, Lost, next to swimming words like Pool Rockets, Lap Swimmers, Breaststrokers, Backstrokers, Sprint Swimmers, Dolphins, Relay Swimmers, Butterflies, Flip Turners, Splash Squad. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
