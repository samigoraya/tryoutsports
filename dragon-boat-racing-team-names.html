    About dragon boat racing

    Dragon boat racing puts up to twenty paddlers in a long decorated boat, with a drummer setting the stroke and a helm steering. It grew from Chinese festival racing into a world championship sport. China, Chinese Taipei, Canada, Germany, Hungary and the Philippines are among the leaders.

    • Team sizeCrews of 10, 20 or 22 including a drummer and a helm
    • Biggest eventsIDBF World Dragon Boat Racing Championships
    • Sport groupWater Sports

    What makes a good dragon boat racing team name?

    Dragon boat racing is a team paddling sport with a drummer and a decorated boat, rooted in Chinese tradition. The best dragon boat racing team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Dragon Paddlers, Drummers or Steersmen, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Dragon Paddlers, Drummers or Steersmen, so the name clearly belongs to dragon boat racing.
    • Say it out loud. A good dragon boat racing team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.
    • Match the tone to the team: fierce for competitive squads, funny or cute for social and youth teams.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A word from the sport itself. For dragon boat racing that means Dragon Paddlers, Drummers or Steersmen, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Alliance, Syndicate, Collective or Brigade. Try your town plus Crew for a dragon boat racing side.
