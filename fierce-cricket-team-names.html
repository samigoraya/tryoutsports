    What makes a good fierce cricket team name?

    Fierce names borrow from predators, storms and heavy metal. They are short, hard-sounding and built to be chanted. For cricket, that means mixing fierce words with the sport itself: Bouncers, Googlies and Yorkers all work well.

    • Pick predators with a strong sound: Vipers, Wolves, Raptors or Sharks.
    • Add weather for power: Thunder, Cyclone, Avalanche or Storm.
    • Use one hard word when you want an intimidating name that is easy to chant.
    • Mix in cricket words like Bouncers, Googlies or Yorkers to tie the name to the sport.

    Fierce words that fit cricket

    Fierce names mix a describing word with a cricket word. Try describing words like Rogue, Iron, Shadow, Red, Savage, Desert, Storm, Midnight, Lightning, Crimson, next to cricket words like Third Men, Free Hits, Slip Cordon, Tailenders, Boundary Hunters, Bouncers, Night Watchmen, Leg Spinners, Googlies, Century Makers. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
