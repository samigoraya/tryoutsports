    What makes a good cute skateboarding team name?

    Cute names use baby animals, sweets and sunshine. They are warm, easy for kids to say and easy to draw. For skateboarding, that means mixing cute words with the sport itself: Skaters, Kickflip Crew and Grind Crew all work well.

    • Choose animals kids love: Bunnies, Ducklings, Pandas or Puppies.
    • Add a friendly word like Happy, Sunny, Fluffy or Tiny.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw so kids can doodle it.
    • Mix in skateboarding words like Skaters, Kickflip Crew or Grind Crew to tie the name to the sport.

    Cute words that fit skateboarding

    Cute names mix a describing word with a skateboarding word. Try describing words like Snuggly, Merry, Little, Mini, Bright, Cheery, Twinkle, Lucky, Jolly, Bouncy, next to skateboarding words like Bowl Riders, Ollie Crew, Kickflip Crew, Park Rats, Trick Masters, Rail Riders, Skaters, Deck Crew, Street Crew, Grind Crew. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
