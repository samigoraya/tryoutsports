    About Para-Cycling

    Para-cycling races on road and track in classes for handcycles, tricycles, bicycles and tandems, where a blind or partially sighted rider is piloted by a sighted teammate. Team sprints and relays add true team medals. Great Britain, the Netherlands, France, Germany, the United States and Australia lead the sport.

    • Team size1 to 3 riders, with team events and tandems
    • Biggest eventsParalympic Games, UCI Para-cycling World Championships
    • Sport groupCycling Sports

    What makes a good Para-Cycling team name?

    Para-cycling includes handcycles, tricycles, tandems and bikes for athletes with a range of impairments. The best Para-Cycling team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Handcyclers, Tandem Crew or Trike Racers, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Handcyclers, Tandem Crew or Trike Racers, so the name clearly belongs to Para-Cycling.
    • Say it out loud. A good Para-Cycling team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.
    • Match the tone to the team: fierce for competitive squads, funny or cute for social and youth teams.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers, Alliance, Athletic or Sporting. Columbus Crew and Philadelphia Union are built this way, and it works for any sport. Try your town plus Crew for a Para-Cycling side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For Para-Cycling that means Handcyclers, Tandem Crew or Trike Racers, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
