    About rugby union

    Rugby union is a contact sport of scrums, lineouts, rucks and mauls, played over two halves of 40 minutes. Points come from tries, conversions, penalties and drop goals. Clubs are the heart of the game, and many carry old town or trade names that have stuck for a century.

    What makes a good rugby union team name?

    Rugby union is a contact sport of scrums, lineouts and rucks, played by teams of fifteen. The best rugby union team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Scrummers, Props or Flankers, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Scrummers, Props or Flankers, so the name clearly belongs to rugby union.
    • Say it out loud. A good rugby union team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers, Alliance, Athletic or Sporting. Columbus Crew and Philadelphia Union are built this way, and it works for any sport. Try your town plus Crew for a rugby union side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For rugby union that means Scrummers, Props or Flankers, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
