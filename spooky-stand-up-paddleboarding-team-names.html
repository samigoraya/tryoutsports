    What makes a good spooky Stand-Up paddleboarding team name?

    Spooky names use ghosts, graveyards and midnight. They are playful rather than scary, and great for Halloween leagues. For Stand-Up paddleboarding, that means mixing spooky words with the sport itself: SUP Squad, Board Balancers and Paddle Pushers all work well.

    • Try ghosts, ghouls, bats and phantoms for a classic spooky feel.
    • Add time words like Midnight, Moonlit or Haunted.
    • Black, purple and orange give a Halloween look.
    • Mix in Stand-Up paddleboarding words like SUP Squad, Board Balancers or Paddle Pushers to tie the name to the sport.
