    What makes a good spooky artistic gymnastics team name?

    Spooky names use ghosts, graveyards and midnight. They are playful rather than scary, and great for Halloween leagues. For artistic gymnastics, that means mixing spooky words with the sport itself: Tumblers, Vaulters and Beam Balancers all work well.

    • Try ghosts, ghouls, bats and phantoms for a classic spooky feel.
    • Add time words like Midnight, Moonlit or Haunted.
    • Black, purple and orange give a Halloween look.
    • Mix in artistic gymnastics words like Tumblers, Vaulters or Beam Balancers to tie the name to the sport.
