    How to choose a team name

    • Say it out loud. A good name is easy to chant from the sideline.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a jersey and a scoreboard.
    • Use your sport's own words, like Googlies for cricket or Alley Oops for basketball.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.
