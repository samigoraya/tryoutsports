    About hang gliding

    Hang gliding flies a rigid-wing glider launched from a hill or by tow, scored on cross-country distance, speed around a course and arrival time. National teams of three to five pilots score together at world meets. Italy, Austria, Switzerland, Brazil, Australia and the Czech Republic are the strongest nations.

    What makes a good hang gliding team name?

    Hang gliding is a free-flight sport where pilots soar on thermals under a fixed wing. The best hang gliding team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Hang Gliders, Thermal Crew or Ridge Soarers, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Hang Gliders, Thermal Crew or Ridge Soarers, so the name clearly belongs to hang gliding.
    • Say it out loud. A good hang gliding team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For hang gliding that means Hang Gliders, Thermal Crew or Ridge Soarers, which tells everyone what you play.
