    What makes a good spooky figure skating team name?

    Spooky names use ghosts, graveyards and midnight. They are playful rather than scary, and great for Halloween leagues. For figure skating, that means mixing spooky words with the sport itself: Skaters, Axel Jumpers and Spinners all work well.

    • Try ghosts, ghouls, bats and phantoms for a classic spooky feel.
    • Add time words like Midnight, Moonlit or Haunted.
    • Black, purple and orange give a Halloween look.
    • Mix in figure skating words like Skaters, Axel Jumpers or Spinners to tie the name to the sport.
