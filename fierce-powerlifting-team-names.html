    What makes a good fierce powerlifting team name?

    Fierce names borrow from predators, storms and heavy metal. They are short, hard-sounding and built to be chanted. For powerlifting, that means mixing fierce words with the sport itself: Powerlifters, Squat Crew and Bench Press Crew all work well.

    • Pick predators with a strong sound: Vipers, Wolves, Raptors or Sharks.
    • Add weather for power: Thunder, Cyclone, Avalanche or Storm.
    • Use one hard word when you want an intimidating name that is easy to chant.
    • Mix in powerlifting words like Powerlifters, Squat Crew or Bench Press Crew to tie the name to the sport.

    Fierce words that fit powerlifting

    Fierce names mix a describing word with a powerlifting word. Try describing words like Midnight, Roaring, Desert, Iron, Black, Rogue, Wild, Night, Red, Howling, next to powerlifting words like Iron Crew, Deadlift Demons, Platform Pros, Plate Pushers, Powerlifters, Rack Pullers, Squat Crew, Total Crew. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
