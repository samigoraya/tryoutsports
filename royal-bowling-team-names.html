    What makes a good royal bowling team name?

    Royal names borrow crowns, castles and noble titles. They sound proud and look sharp on gold-trimmed kit. For bowling, that means mixing royal words with the sport itself: Strikes, Spares and Turkeys all work well.

    • Use titles like Kings, Queens, Barons, Knights or Monarchs.
    • Add crowns, castles and thrones for a regal image.
    • Gold, purple and deep red are the classic royal colors.
    • Mix in bowling words like Strikes, Spares or Turkeys to tie the name to the sport.

    Royal words that fit bowling

    Royal names mix a describing word with a bowling word. Try describing words like Velvet, Purple, Crowned, Honored, Noble, Exalted, Silver, High, Supreme, Grand, next to bowling words like Rollers, Pin Crashers, Alley Cats, Pin Pals, Strikes, Turkeys, Spares, Lane Legends, Hook Shots, Perfect Games. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
