    What makes a good royal pole dancing team name?

    Royal names borrow crowns, castles and noble titles. They sound proud and look sharp on gold-trimmed kit. For pole dancing, that means mixing royal words with the sport itself: Pole Stars, Spin Queens and Pole Flyers all work well.

    • Use titles like Kings, Queens, Barons, Knights or Monarchs.
    • Add crowns, castles and thrones for a regal image.
    • Gold, purple and deep red are the classic royal colors.
    • Mix in pole dancing words like Pole Stars, Spin Queens or Pole Flyers to tie the name to the sport.
