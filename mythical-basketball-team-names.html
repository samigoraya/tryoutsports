    What makes a good mythical basketball team name?

    Mythical names draw on dragons, phoenixes, titans and legends. They feel epic and make great logos. For basketball, that means mixing mythical words with the sport itself: Dunkers, Rebounders and Shooters all work well.

    • Pick a creature with a strong silhouette: Dragons, Griffins or Phoenixes.
    • Add a mythic word like Ancient, Immortal, Eternal or Legend.
    • Borrow from the myths of your own region for a local twist.
    • Mix in basketball words like Dunkers, Rebounders or Shooters to tie the name to the sport.

    Mythical words that fit basketball

    Mythical names mix a describing word with a basketball word. Try describing words like Enchanted, Storm, Sacred, Flame, Ancient, Elder, Golden, Shadow, Mystic, Crystal, next to basketball words like Pivots, Fadeaways, Dribblers, Sixth Men, Slashers, Hoopsters, Layups, Tip Offs, Dunkers, Lockdown Defense. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
