    What makes a good royal free diving team name?

    Royal names borrow crowns, castles and noble titles. They sound proud and look sharp on gold-trimmed kit. For free diving, that means mixing royal words with the sport itself: Free Divers, Breath Holders and Depth Seekers all work well.

    • Use titles like Kings, Queens, Barons, Knights or Monarchs.
    • Add crowns, castles and thrones for a regal image.
    • Gold, purple and deep red are the classic royal colors.
    • Mix in free diving words like Free Divers, Breath Holders or Depth Seekers to tie the name to the sport.
