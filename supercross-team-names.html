    About supercross

    Supercross is motocross brought indoors into stadiums, on tight man-made tracks with big jumps and whoops. Riders race for factory teams through a winter and spring series. It is biggest in the United States, with a growing world championship.

    What makes a good supercross team name?

    Supercross is stadium motocross on man-made tracks full of huge jumps and rhythm sections. The best supercross team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Stadium Riders, Rhythm Masters or Triple Crown, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Stadium Riders, Rhythm Masters or Triple Crown, so the name clearly belongs to supercross.
    • Say it out loud. A good supercross team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.
    • Match the tone to the team: fierce for competitive squads, funny or cute for social and youth teams.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A word from the sport itself. For supercross that means Stadium Riders, Rhythm Masters or Triple Crown, which tells everyone what you play.
