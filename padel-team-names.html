    About padel

    Padel is doubles-only, played in a glass-and-mesh cage with a solid bat and an underarm serve, using tennis scoring. The walls keep rallies alive, so beginners can play a real point on day one. Spain and Argentina are the heartlands, and it is the fastest-growing racquet sport in Europe and the Middle East.

    • Team size2 a side, always doubles
    • Biggest eventsPadel World Championship, Premier Padel
    • Sport groupRacquet & Paddle Sports

    What makes a good padel team name?

    Padel is a doubles racquet sport played in a glass-walled court, mixing tennis with squash-style wall play. The best padel team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Bandejas, Vibora Hitters or Wall Rebounders, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Bandejas, Vibora Hitters or Wall Rebounders, so the name clearly belongs to padel.
    • Say it out loud. A good padel team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.
    • Match the tone to the team: fierce for competitive squads, funny or cute for social and youth teams.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Alliance, Syndicate, Collective or Brigade. Try your town plus Crew for a padel side.
    • A word from the sport itself. For padel that means Bandejas, Vibora Hitters or Wall Rebounders, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
