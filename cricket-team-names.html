    What makes a good cricket team name?

    Cricket is a bat-and-ball game of overs, wickets and runs, played from village greens to packed stadiums. The best cricket team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Bouncers, Googlies or Yorkers, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Bouncers, Googlies or Yorkers, so the name clearly belongs to cricket.
    • Say it out loud. A good cricket team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A word from the sport itself. For cricket that means Bouncers, Googlies or Yorkers, which tells everyone what you play.
    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers, Alliance, Athletic or Sporting. Try your town plus Crew for a cricket side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
