    What makes a good mythical scrabble team name?

    Mythical names draw on dragons, phoenixes, titans and legends. They feel epic and make great logos. For scrabble, that means mixing mythical words with the sport itself: Word Crew, Tile Turners and Triple Word Crew all work well.

    • Pick a creature with a strong silhouette: Dragons, Griffins or Phoenixes.
    • Add a mythic word like Ancient, Immortal, Eternal or Legend.
    • Borrow from the myths of your own region for a local twist.
    • Mix in scrabble words like Word Crew, Tile Turners or Triple Word Crew to tie the name to the sport.
