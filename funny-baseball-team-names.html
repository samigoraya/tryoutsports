    What makes a good funny baseball team name?

    The best funny names turn a baseball word into a joke: a pun, an odd animal or a snack. If it makes your own team laugh at the first practice, it works.

    • Twist a baseball term: Foul Poles, Pop Flies, Bat Flips, Wild Pitches.
    • Add food for an easy laugh: Corn Dogs, Waffles, Flapjacks, Nachos.
    • Put an adjective on the front, like Sleepy, Soggy or Wobbly.
    • Check that the joke still reads well when it is printed on a jersey.

    Questions people ask

    What is a funny name for a baseball team?

    Puns on baseball words are the safest bet, for example Bunt Cakes, Pine Tar Pandas or Grand Salamis. Food names such as Pickle Jars or Corn Dogs also work well.

    Are funny names allowed in a serious league?

    Usually yes, but check your league rules on team names first. Some leagues ask for approval before the roster is registered.

    How can I make a funny team name more personal?

    Add your hometown or a short description in the boxes above. A place at the end, like Dancing Catfish Karachi, ties the joke to your team.
