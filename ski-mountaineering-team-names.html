    About ski mountaineering

    Ski mountaineering, or skimo, climbs on skis with skins and races back down, over sprint, individual, vertical and team formats. Teams of two or three stay roped together in the classic race format. It joins the Winter Olympic programme in 2026, and Italy, France, Switzerland, Spain and Austria lead the sport.

    • Team size1 athlete, with mixed relays of 2 to 4
    • Biggest eventsISMF World Championships, Winter Olympics from 2026
    • Sport groupWinter Sports

    What makes a good ski mountaineering team name?

    Ski mountaineering, or skimo, combines uphill climbs on skis with fast downhill descents. The best ski mountaineering team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Skimo Squad, Skin Climbers or Summit Skiers, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Skimo Squad, Skin Climbers or Summit Skiers, so the name clearly belongs to ski mountaineering.
    • Say it out loud. A good ski mountaineering team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers, Alliance, Athletic or Sporting. Columbus Crew and Philadelphia Union are built this way, and it works for any sport. Try your town plus Crew for a ski mountaineering side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For ski mountaineering that means Skimo Squad, Skin Climbers or Summit Skiers, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
