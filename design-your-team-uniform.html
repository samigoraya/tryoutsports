Questions people ask
How much does a custom team uniform cost?
It depends on the fabric, the number of pieces and the printing method. Send the form with your sport, quantity and colors and TRYOUT Sports will come back with a quote.
What is the minimum order?
Tell us your team size in the form. Small squads and full clubs are both fine, and the quote will show the price at your quantity.
Do I see the design before it is made?
Yes. TRYOUT Sports sends a mockup of your uniform and only starts production once you approve it.
Can you put the team name from the generator on the uniform?
Yes. Copy the name you picked into the form and it goes on the mockup with your colors and numbers.
