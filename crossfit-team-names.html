    What makes a good CrossFit team name?

    CrossFit is a high-intensity fitness sport of varied functional movements, from lifting to gymnastics. The best CrossFit team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as WOD Warriors, Box Crew or AMRAP Crew, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like WOD Warriors, Box Crew or AMRAP Crew, so the name clearly belongs to CrossFit.
    • Say it out loud. A good CrossFit team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For CrossFit that means WOD Warriors, Box Crew or AMRAP Crew, which tells everyone what you play.
    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Alliance, Syndicate, Collective or Brigade. Try your town plus Crew for a CrossFit side.
