    What makes a good classic volleyball team name?

    Classic names lean on tradition, trades and hometown pride. They are short, timeless and look good stitched across a jersey. For volleyball, that means mixing classic words with the sport itself: Spikers, Setters and Liberos all work well.

    • Pair your town with a trade or tradition, like Millers, Pioneers or Railmen.
    • Keep it to two words so it fits a jersey and reads clearly on a scoreboard.
    • Pick a name that will still sound good in ten years.
    • Mix in volleyball words like Spikers, Setters or Liberos to tie the name to the sport.

    Classic words that fit volleyball

    Classic names mix a describing word with a volleyball word. Try describing words like Coastal, Old, Prairie, Dusty, Evening, Union, Silver, Harbor, River, Canyon, next to volleyball words like Diggers, Rally Scorers, Liberos, Hitters, Middle Blockers, Kills, Setters, Spikers, Blockers, Sideouts. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
