    What makes a good mythical rock climbing team name?

    Mythical names draw on dragons, phoenixes, titans and legends. They feel epic and make great logos. For rock climbing, that means mixing mythical words with the sport itself: Climbers, Boulder Crew and Crag Crew all work well.

    • Pick a creature with a strong silhouette: Dragons, Griffins or Phoenixes.
    • Add a mythic word like Ancient, Immortal, Eternal or Legend.
    • Borrow from the myths of your own region for a local twist.
    • Mix in rock climbing words like Climbers, Boulder Crew or Crag Crew to tie the name to the sport.

    Mythical words that fit rock climbing

    Mythical names mix a describing word with a rock climbing word. Try describing words like Legendary, Immortal, Sacred, Storm, Emerald, Enchanted, Thunder, Moonlit, Arcane, Mystic, next to rock climbing words like Crag Crew, Crimp Crew, Chalk Crew, Rope Crew, Route Setters, Summit Seekers, Boulder Crew, Cliff Crew, Wall Crew, Belay Crew. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
