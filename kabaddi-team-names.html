    About kabaddi

    Kabaddi is a South Asian contact sport where a raider enters the other half, tries to tag defenders and get back over the line without being tackled. No ball and no equipment are needed, just a mat or a patch of ground. India, Iran, Pakistan, Bangladesh, South Korea and Kenya all compete seriously.

    • Team size7 a side, with raiders and defenders
    • Biggest eventsKabaddi World Cup, Pro Kabaddi League, Asian Games

    What makes a good kabaddi team name?

    Kabaddi is a South Asian contact sport where raiders try to tag defenders and return without being tackled. The best kabaddi team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Raiders, Defenders or Tacklers, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Raiders, Defenders or Tacklers, so the name clearly belongs to kabaddi.
    • Say it out loud. A good kabaddi team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For kabaddi that means Raiders, Defenders or Tacklers, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
