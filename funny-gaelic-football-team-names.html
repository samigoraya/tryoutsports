    What makes a good funny Gaelic football team name?

    The best funny names turn a word from the sport into a joke: a pun, an odd animal or a snack. If it makes your own team laugh, it works. For Gaelic football, that means mixing funny words with the sport itself: Point Scorers, Goal Getters and Hand Passers all work well.

    • Twist a word from your sport into a pun.
    • Add food for an easy laugh: Waffles, Pickles or Nachos.
    • Put a goofy adjective on the front: Sleepy, Soggy or Wobbly.
    • Mix in Gaelic football words like Point Scorers, Goal Getters or Hand Passers to tie the name to the sport.
