    What makes a good spooky race walking team name?

    Spooky names use ghosts, graveyards and midnight. They are playful rather than scary, and great for Halloween leagues. For race walking, that means mixing spooky words with the sport itself: Walkers, Heel Toe Heroes and Straight Knees all work well.

    • Try ghosts, ghouls, bats and phantoms for a classic spooky feel.
    • Add time words like Midnight, Moonlit or Haunted.
    • Black, purple and orange give a Halloween look.
    • Mix in race walking words like Walkers, Heel Toe Heroes or Straight Knees to tie the name to the sport.

    Spooky words that fit race walking

    Spooky names mix a describing word with a race walking word. Try describing words like Dark, Cursed, Shadow, Vanishing, Spooky, Haunted, Grim, Ghostly, Howling, Eerie, next to race walking words like Gait Gurus, Walkers, Contact Keepers, Judges Eyes, Straight Knees, Fast Feet, Stride Masters, Pace Walkers, Road Walkers. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
