    What makes a good mythical Off-Road racing team name?

    Mythical names draw on dragons, phoenixes, titans and legends. They feel epic and make great logos. For Off-Road racing, that means mixing mythical words with the sport itself: Desert Racers, Baja Brigade and Trophy Trucks all work well.

    • Pick a creature with a strong silhouette: Dragons, Griffins or Phoenixes.
    • Add a mythic word like Ancient, Immortal, Eternal or Legend.
    • Borrow from the myths of your own region for a local twist.
    • Mix in Off-Road racing words like Desert Racers, Baja Brigade or Trophy Trucks to tie the name to the sport.

    Mythical words that fit Off-Road racing

    Mythical names mix a describing word with a Off-Road racing word. Try describing words like Arcane, Thunder, Storm, Moonlit, Enchanted, Legendary, Elder, Frost, Shadow, Mystic, next to Off-Road racing words like Dust Devils, Mud Plugger, Rock Crawlers, Dune Runners, Dirt Crew, Baja Brigade, Trophy Trucks, Buggy Boys, Terrain Tamers, Desert Racers. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
