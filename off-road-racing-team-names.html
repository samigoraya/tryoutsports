    About Off-Road racing

    Off-road racing covers desert and short-course events where trucks and buggies cross hundreds of miles of rough ground. Chase crews, navigators and mechanics are as important as the driver. Mexico, the United States, Saudi Arabia, France and South Africa host the most famous events.

    • Team size1 to 3 drivers per vehicle, with big crews
    • Biggest eventsBaja 1000, Dakar Rally, King of the Hammers
    • Sport groupMotorsports

    What makes a good Off-Road racing team name?

    Off-road racing puts trucks, buggies and bikes against deserts, mud and rocks. The best Off-Road racing team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Desert Racers, Baja Brigade or Trophy Trucks, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Desert Racers, Baja Brigade or Trophy Trucks, so the name clearly belongs to Off-Road racing.
    • Say it out loud. A good Off-Road racing team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Alliance, Syndicate, Collective or Brigade. Try your town plus Crew for a Off-Road racing side.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For Off-Road racing that means Desert Racers, Baja Brigade or Trophy Trucks, which tells everyone what you play.
