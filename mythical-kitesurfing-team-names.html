    What makes a good mythical kitesurfing team name?

    Mythical names draw on dragons, phoenixes, titans and legends. They feel epic and make great logos. For kitesurfing, that means mixing mythical words with the sport itself: Kiters, Air Riders and Big Air Boys all work well.

    • Pick a creature with a strong silhouette: Dragons, Griffins or Phoenixes.
    • Add a mythic word like Ancient, Immortal, Eternal or Legend.
    • Borrow from the myths of your own region for a local twist.
    • Mix in kitesurfing words like Kiters, Air Riders or Big Air Boys to tie the name to the sport.

    Mythical words that fit kitesurfing

    Mythical names mix a describing word with a kitesurfing word. Try describing words like Emerald, Thunder, Arcane, Frost, Shadow, Enchanted, Fabled, Sacred, Immortal, Crystal, next to kitesurfing words like Board Flyers, Power Kites, Sky Dancers, Jump Kings, Kiters, Wind Whippers, Lift Seekers, Loop Masters, Kite Shredders, Air Riders. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
