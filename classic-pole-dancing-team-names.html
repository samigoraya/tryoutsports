    What makes a good classic pole dancing team name?

    Classic names lean on tradition, trades and hometown pride. They are short, timeless and look good stitched across a jersey. For pole dancing, that means mixing classic words with the sport itself: Pole Stars, Spin Queens and Pole Flyers all work well.

    • Pair your town with a trade or tradition, like Millers, Pioneers or Railmen.
    • Keep it to two words so it fits a jersey and reads clearly on a scoreboard.
    • Pick a name that will still sound good in ten years.
    • Mix in pole dancing words like Pole Stars, Spin Queens or Pole Flyers to tie the name to the sport.

    Classic words that fit pole dancing

    Classic names mix a describing word with a pole dancing word. Try describing words like Evening, Iron, Mountain, Summer, Silver, Copper, Dusty, Harbor, River, Prairie, next to pole dancing words like Shine Squad, Inverted Crew, Pole Flyers, Climb Crew, Flex Crew, Pole Stars, Pole Pros, Strength Dancers, Spin Queens, Aerial Dancers. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
