    About karate

    Karate is a Japanese striking art practised in kata, the solo form, and kumite, the sparring. Team kata and team kumite put three or five club-mates together under one name. Japan, France, Spain, Italy, Turkey and Egypt dominate at world level.

    What makes a good karate team name?

    Karate is an Okinawan-Japanese martial art of striking, with kata forms and sparring called kumite. The best karate team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Kata Masters, Kumite Kings or Black Belts, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Kata Masters, Kumite Kings or Black Belts, so the name clearly belongs to karate.
    • Say it out loud. A good karate team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.
    • Match the tone to the team: fierce for competitive squads, funny or cute for social and youth teams.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Alliance, Syndicate, Collective or Brigade. Try your town plus Crew for a karate side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A word from the sport itself. For karate that means Kata Masters, Kumite Kings or Black Belts, which tells everyone what you play.
