    About NASCAR

    NASCAR races heavy stock cars on ovals and some road courses, with drafting, pit strategy and contact all part of it. Most drivers belong to multi-car organisations that share data and engines. It is the biggest motorsport in the United States, with the Daytona 500 as its showpiece.

    What makes a good NASCAR team name?

    NASCAR is stock car racing around ovals, road courses and superspeedways. The best NASCAR team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Stock Cars, Draft Masters or Pit Crew, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Stock Cars, Draft Masters or Pit Crew, so the name clearly belongs to NASCAR.
    • Say it out loud. A good NASCAR team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers, Alliance, Athletic or Sporting. Columbus Crew and Philadelphia Union are built this way, and it works for any sport. Try your town plus Crew for a NASCAR side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For NASCAR that means Stock Cars, Draft Masters or Pit Crew, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
