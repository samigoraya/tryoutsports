    About soccer

    Soccer is the most played team sport in the world, with two teams of eleven trying to put the ball in the net over two halves of 45 minutes. Only the goalkeeper may use the hands inside the penalty area. Clubs run from village sides to global giants, and most countries have a league pyramid that any new team can enter at the bottom.

    What makes a good soccer team name?

    Soccer, called football in most of the world, is played on grass pitches from school fields to World Cup stadiums. The best soccer team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Strikers, Midfielders or Defenders, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Strikers, Midfielders or Defenders, so the name clearly belongs to soccer.
    • Say it out loud. A good soccer team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Match the tone to the team: fierce for competitive squads, funny or cute for social and youth teams.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For soccer that means Strikers, Midfielders or Defenders, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
