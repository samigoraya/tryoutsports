    About scrabble

    Scrabble scores words placed on a 15 by 15 board, with bonus squares and a 50-point bonus for using all seven tiles. Club and national team matches are common, especially in the United Kingdom, Nigeria and Thailand. Nigeria, the United States, Thailand, Australia and the United Kingdom are among the strongest nations.

    What makes a good scrabble team name?

    Scrabble is a word tile game played at home and in clubs, where vocabulary and tile play win games. The best scrabble team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Word Crew, Tile Turners or Triple Word Crew, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Word Crew, Tile Turners or Triple Word Crew, so the name clearly belongs to scrabble.
    • Say it out loud. A good scrabble team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.
    • Match the tone to the team: fierce for competitive squads, funny or cute for social and youth teams.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A word from the sport itself. For scrabble that means Word Crew, Tile Turners or Triple Word Crew, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
