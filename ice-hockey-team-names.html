    About ice hockey

    Ice hockey is played on skates with sticks and a puck across three 20-minute periods. Shifts last under a minute, so whole lines change on the fly. Canada, the United States, Sweden, Finland, Russia and the Czech Republic are the traditional powers, and local rinks run leagues for every age.

    What makes a good ice hockey team name?

    Ice hockey is played on skates at high speed, with sticks, a puck and constant line changes. The best ice hockey team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Slap Shots, Power Plays or Face Offs, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Slap Shots, Power Plays or Face Offs, so the name clearly belongs to ice hockey.
    • Say it out loud. A good ice hockey team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Match the tone to the team: fierce for competitive squads, funny or cute for social and youth teams.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A word from the sport itself. For ice hockey that means Slap Shots, Power Plays or Face Offs, which tells everyone what you play.
