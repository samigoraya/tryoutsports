    About Stand-Up paddleboarding

    Stand-up paddleboarding is raced in sprints, technical races and long distance, and also surfed in waves. It is one of the easiest water sports to start, so clubs form fast around lakes and coastlines. France, the United States, Australia, Brazil and Spain lead the racing.

    What makes a good Stand-Up paddleboarding team name?

    Stand-up paddleboarding is a calm or racing water sport on a large board with a single paddle. The best Stand-Up paddleboarding team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as SUP Squad, Board Balancers or Paddle Pushers, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like SUP Squad, Board Balancers or Paddle Pushers, so the name clearly belongs to Stand-Up paddleboarding.
    • Say it out loud. A good Stand-Up paddleboarding team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.
    • Match the tone to the team: fierce for competitive squads, funny or cute for social and youth teams.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
    • A word from the sport itself. For Stand-Up paddleboarding that means SUP Squad, Board Balancers or Paddle Pushers, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
