    About judo

    Judo is a Japanese grappling art of throws, holds, pins and submissions, where a clean ippon ends the contest at once. Clubs train together and enter team events of three to six fighters. Japan, France, Georgia, Brazil, Russia and South Korea lead the sport.

    What makes a good judo team name?

    Judo is a Japanese martial art built around throws, pins and submissions, using balance and leverage. The best judo team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Ippons, Throwers or Seoi Nage, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Ippons, Throwers or Seoi Nage, so the name clearly belongs to judo.
    • Say it out loud. A good judo team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A word from the sport itself. For judo that means Ippons, Throwers or Seoi Nage, which tells everyone what you play.
