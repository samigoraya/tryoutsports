    About BMX

    BMX splits into racing, where eight riders sprint a jump-filled track, and freestyle, judged on tricks in a park. Both joined the Olympics, racing in 2008 and freestyle in 2021. The United States, France, the Netherlands, Australia, Colombia and Great Britain lead the sport.

    What makes a good BMX team name?

    BMX covers racing and freestyle, with bikes built for jumps, tricks and speed. The best BMX team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as BMX Riders, Gate Droppers or Berm Blasters, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like BMX Riders, Gate Droppers or Berm Blasters, so the name clearly belongs to BMX.
    • Say it out loud. A good BMX team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers, Alliance, Athletic or Sporting. Columbus Crew and Philadelphia Union are built this way, and it works for any sport. Try your town plus Crew for a BMX side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For BMX that means BMX Riders, Gate Droppers or Berm Blasters, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
