    About snooker

    Snooker is played on a twelve-foot table with 21 balls, building breaks by potting reds and colours alternately, with 147 the maximum. Matches run to a best of nine frames up to a best of 35 at the World Championship. England, China, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland and Australia dominate.

    What makes a good snooker team name?

    Snooker is a cue sport of potting reds and colours, known for patience and tactical play. The best snooker team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Frame Winners, Century Makers or Pot Masters, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Frame Winners, Century Makers or Pot Masters, so the name clearly belongs to snooker.
    • Say it out loud. A good snooker team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Alliance, Syndicate, Collective or Brigade. Try your town plus Crew for a snooker side.
    • A word from the sport itself. For snooker that means Frame Winners, Century Makers or Pot Masters, which tells everyone what you play.
