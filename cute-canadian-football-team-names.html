    What makes a good cute Canadian football team name?

    Cute names use baby animals, sweets and sunshine. They are warm, easy for kids to say and easy to draw. For Canadian football, that means mixing cute words with the sport itself: Rouges, Receivers and Linemen all work well.

    • Choose animals kids love: Bunnies, Ducklings, Pandas or Puppies.
    • Add a friendly word like Happy, Sunny, Fluffy or Tiny.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw so kids can doodle it.
    • Mix in Canadian football words like Rouges, Receivers or Linemen to tie the name to the sport.

    Cute words that fit Canadian football

    Cute names mix a describing word with a Canadian football word. Try describing words like Cozy, Sparkly, Bouncy, Tiny, Jolly, Snuggly, Fuzzy, Sweet, Merry, Lucky, next to Canadian football words like Quarterbacks, Blitzers, Huddlers, Receivers, End Zone, Snap Counters, Kickers, Backfield, Touchdowns, Hail Marys. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
