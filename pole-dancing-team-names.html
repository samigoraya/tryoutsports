    About pole dancing

    Pole sports is judged on strength, flexibility and artistry in routines on a static and a spinning pole. Doubles events pair two athletes, and studios enter whole teams at competitions. Russia, Ukraine, Great Britain, Italy, Australia and the United States are among the leading nations.

    What makes a good pole dancing team name?

    Pole sport blends strength, flexibility and dance on a vertical pole, in fitness and competition. The best pole dancing team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Pole Stars, Spin Queens or Pole Flyers, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Pole Stars, Spin Queens or Pole Flyers, so the name clearly belongs to pole dancing.
    • Say it out loud. A good pole dancing team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Alliance, Syndicate, Collective or Brigade. Try your town plus Crew for a pole dancing side.
    • A word from the sport itself. For pole dancing that means Pole Stars, Spin Queens or Pole Flyers, which tells everyone what you play.
