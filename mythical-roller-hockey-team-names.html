    What makes a good mythical roller hockey team name?

    Mythical names draw on dragons, phoenixes, titans and legends. They feel epic and make great logos. For roller hockey, that means mixing mythical words with the sport itself: Rollers, Wheelers and Skaters all work well.

    • Pick a creature with a strong silhouette: Dragons, Griffins or Phoenixes.
    • Add a mythic word like Ancient, Immortal, Eternal or Legend.
    • Borrow from the myths of your own region for a local twist.
    • Mix in roller hockey words like Rollers, Wheelers or Skaters to tie the name to the sport.

    Mythical words that fit roller hockey

    Mythical names mix a describing word with a roller hockey word. Try describing words like Enchanted, Arcane, Sacred, Thunder, Eternal, Ancient, Legendary, Fabled, Storm, Frost, next to roller hockey words like Pad Savers, Power Plays, Wheelers, Rollers, Quads, Wheel Kings, Goalies, Stick Handlers, Speed Skaters, Inliners. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
