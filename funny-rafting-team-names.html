    What makes a good funny rafting team name?

    The best funny names turn a word from the sport into a joke: a pun, an odd animal or a snack. If it makes your own team laugh, it works. For rafting, that means mixing funny words with the sport itself: Rafters, Rapid Runners and Whitewater Warriors all work well.

    • Twist a word from your sport into a pun.
    • Add food for an easy laugh: Waffles, Pickles or Nachos.
    • Put a goofy adjective on the front: Sleepy, Soggy or Wobbly.
    • Mix in rafting words like Rafters, Rapid Runners or Whitewater Warriors to tie the name to the sport.

    Funny words that fit rafting

    Funny names mix a describing word with a rafting word. Try describing words like Slightly, Dizzy, Clumsy, Sticky, Sunburnt, Fancy, Loud, Late, Wandering, Sneezing, next to rafting words like River Guides, Rapid Runners, Paddle Crew, Canyon Crew, Rapids Rats, Eddy Hunters, Whitewater Warriors, Raft Riders, Wave Crashers, Rafters. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
