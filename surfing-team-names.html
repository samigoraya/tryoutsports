    About surfing

    Surfing is scored on the waves a rider catches in a timed heat, judged on power, flow and manoeuvres. The ISA world games are contested by national teams, and most surfers belong to a local board-riders club. Australia, Brazil, the United States, Japan, Portugal and South Africa lead the sport.

    What makes a good surfing team name?

    Surfing is riding ocean waves on a board, from beginners to big-wave chargers. The best surfing team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Wave Riders, Barrel Hunters or Swell Seekers, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Wave Riders, Barrel Hunters or Swell Seekers, so the name clearly belongs to surfing.
    • Say it out loud. A good surfing team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Alliance, Syndicate, Collective or Brigade. Try your town plus Crew for a surfing side.
    • A word from the sport itself. For surfing that means Wave Riders, Barrel Hunters or Swell Seekers, which tells everyone what you play.
