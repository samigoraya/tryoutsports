    What makes a good mythical Cyclo-Cross team name?

    Mythical names draw on dragons, phoenixes, titans and legends. They feel epic and make great logos. For Cyclo-Cross, that means mixing mythical words with the sport itself: Mud Riders, Barrier Hoppers and Run Up Crew all work well.

    • Pick a creature with a strong silhouette: Dragons, Griffins or Phoenixes.
    • Add a mythic word like Ancient, Immortal, Eternal or Legend.
    • Borrow from the myths of your own region for a local twist.
    • Mix in Cyclo-Cross words like Mud Riders, Barrier Hoppers or Run Up Crew to tie the name to the sport.
