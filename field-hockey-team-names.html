    About field hockey

    Field hockey is a stick-and-ball game of quick passing, penalty corners and drag flicks, played over four quarters. The Netherlands, Australia, Belgium, India, Germany and Argentina lead the sport. School and club hockey is especially strong in Europe, South Asia and Oceania.

    What makes a good field hockey team name?

    Field hockey is a stick-and-ball game played on grass or turf, known for penalty corners and quick passing. The best field hockey team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Drag Flickers, Penalty Corners or Sticks, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Drag Flickers, Penalty Corners or Sticks, so the name clearly belongs to field hockey.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers, Alliance, Athletic or Sporting. Try your town plus Crew for a field hockey side.
    • A word from the sport itself. For field hockey that means Drag Flickers, Penalty Corners or Sticks, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
