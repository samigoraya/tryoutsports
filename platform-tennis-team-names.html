    About platform tennis

    Platform tennis, or paddle, is played outdoors in winter on a heated aluminium deck surrounded by wire screens, with the screens in play. Teams are pairs, and clubs run leagues through the cold months. It is mostly played in the northern United States.

    • Team size2 a side, always doubles, in winter
    • Biggest eventsAPTA National Championships

    Platform tennis is a cold-weather paddle game played on a raised, heated platform with a screened wall. The best platform tennis team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Screen Players, Paddle Winters or Deck Dwellers, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers, Alliance, Athletic or Sporting. Columbus Crew and Philadelphia Union are built this way, and it works for any sport. Try your town plus Crew for a platform tennis side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For platform tennis that means Screen Players, Paddle Winters or Deck Dwellers, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
