    About Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

    Brazilian jiu-jitsu is a grappling art built on leverage, positions and submissions, practised in gi and no-gi. Tournaments award team points, so the academy name on the back of the gi matters. Brazil and the United States lead, with strong scenes in the United Arab Emirates, Japan, Russia and Europe.

    What makes a good Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu team name?

    Brazilian jiu-jitsu is a ground-fighting art focused on positions, joint locks and chokes. The best Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Guard Passers, Sweepers or Arm Bars, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Guard Passers, Sweepers or Arm Bars, so the name clearly belongs to Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu.
    • Say it out loud. A good Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
    • A word from the sport itself. For Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu that means Guard Passers, Sweepers or Arm Bars, which tells everyone what you play.
