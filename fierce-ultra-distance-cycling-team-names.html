    What makes a good fierce Ultra-Distance cycling team name?

    Fierce names borrow from predators, storms and heavy metal. They are short, hard-sounding and built to be chanted. For Ultra-Distance cycling, that means mixing fierce words with the sport itself: Ultra Riders, Bikepackers and Night Riders all work well.

    • Pick predators with a strong sound: Vipers, Wolves, Raptors or Sharks.
    • Add weather for power: Thunder, Cyclone, Avalanche or Storm.
    • Use one hard word when you want an intimidating name that is easy to chant.
    • Mix in Ultra-Distance cycling words like Ultra Riders, Bikepackers or Night Riders to tie the name to the sport.
