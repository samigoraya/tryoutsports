    What makes a good spooky rugby union team name?

    Spooky names use ghosts, graveyards and midnight. They are playful rather than scary, and great for Halloween leagues. For rugby union, that means mixing spooky words with the sport itself: Scrummers, Props and Flankers all work well.

    • Try ghosts, ghouls, bats and phantoms for a classic spooky feel.
    • Add time words like Midnight, Moonlit or Haunted.
    • Black, purple and orange give a Halloween look.
    • Mix in rugby union words like Scrummers, Props or Flankers to tie the name to the sport.

    Spooky words that fit rugby union

    Spooky names mix a describing word with a rugby union word. Try describing words like Gloomy, Dark, Shadow, Spooky, Vanishing, Howling, Phantom, Moonlit, Midnight, Wicked, next to rugby union words like Forwards, Centres, Scrummers, Garryowens, Front Row, Fullbacks, Sidesteps, Drop Goals, Tries, Breakdowns. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
