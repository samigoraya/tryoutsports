    What makes a good spooky hurdles team name?

    Spooky names use ghosts, graveyards and midnight. They are playful rather than scary, and great for Halloween leagues. For hurdles, that means mixing spooky words with the sport itself: Hurdlers, Lead Legs and Trail Legs all work well.

    • Try ghosts, ghouls, bats and phantoms for a classic spooky feel.
    • Add time words like Midnight, Moonlit or Haunted.
    • Black, purple and orange give a Halloween look.
    • Mix in hurdles words like Hurdlers, Lead Legs or Trail Legs to tie the name to the sport.

    Spooky words that fit hurdles

    Spooky names mix a describing word with a hurdles word. Try describing words like Hollow, Gloomy, Cursed, Foggy, Creepy, Shadow, Spooky, Howling, Screaming, Vanishing, next to hurdles words like Clearing Kings, Hurdle Hoppers, Flat Runners, Steeplechasers, Trail Legs, Rhythm Runners, Barrier Breakers, High Steppers, Lead Legs, Hurdlers. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
