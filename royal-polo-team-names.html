    What makes a good royal polo team name?

    Royal names borrow crowns, castles and noble titles. They sound proud and look sharp on gold-trimmed kit. For polo, that means mixing royal words with the sport itself: Mallet Masters, Chukker Champs and Pony Players all work well.

    • Use titles like Kings, Queens, Barons, Knights or Monarchs.
    • Add crowns, castles and thrones for a regal image.
    • Gold, purple and deep red are the classic royal colors.
    • Mix in polo words like Mallet Masters, Chukker Champs or Pony Players to tie the name to the sport.

    Royal words that fit polo

    Royal names mix a describing word with a polo word. Try describing words like Imperial, Silver, Gallant, Supreme, Honored, Valiant, Crowned, Sovereign, Golden, Gilded, next to polo words like Pony Players, Mallet Masters, Polo Ponies, Ride Offs, Goal Hitters, Chukker Champs, Back Hands, Neck Shots, Field Flyers. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
