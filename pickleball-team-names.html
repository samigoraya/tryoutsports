    About pickleball

    Pickleball is played on a small court with a perforated plastic ball and solid paddles, with the serve hit underhand and a no-volley kitchen in front of the net. It is easy on the body and very social, which has made it the fastest-growing sport in the United States. Club and league teams form quickly around it.

    What makes a good pickleball team name?

    Pickleball is a paddle game that mixes tennis, badminton and ping pong on a small court with a plastic ball. The best pickleball team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Dinkers, Picklers or Kitchen Kings, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Dinkers, Picklers or Kitchen Kings, so the name clearly belongs to pickleball.
    • Say it out loud. A good pickleball team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers, Alliance, Athletic or Sporting. Columbus Crew and Philadelphia Union are built this way, and it works for any sport. Try your town plus Crew for a pickleball side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For pickleball that means Dinkers, Picklers or Kitchen Kings, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
