    What makes a good space aikido team name?

    Space names borrow rockets, planets and galaxies. They sound fast and futuristic and look great with metallic colors. For aikido, that means mixing space words with the sport itself: Aikidokas, Ki Masters and Blend Masters all work well.

    • Use planets and objects: Comets, Meteors, Rockets or Nebulas.
    • Add a futuristic word like Cosmic, Stellar, Orbital or Quantum.
    • Silver, navy and electric blue match the theme.
    • Mix in aikido words like Aikidokas, Ki Masters or Blend Masters to tie the name to the sport.

    Space words that fit aikido

    Space names mix a describing word with a aikido word. Try describing words like Quantum, Galactic, Atomic, Nova, Sonic, Rocket, Solar, Lunar, Laser, Astro, next to aikido words like Wrist Locks, Harmonizers, Way Walkers, Flow Fighters, Circle Throwers, Ki Masters, Balance Breakers, Aikidokas, Dojo Dancers, Hakama Heroes. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
