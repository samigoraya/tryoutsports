    What makes a good royal archery team name?

    Royal names borrow crowns, castles and noble titles. They sound proud and look sharp on gold-trimmed kit. For archery, that means mixing royal words with the sport itself: Archers, Bullseyes and Arrow Slingers all work well.

    • Use titles like Kings, Queens, Barons, Knights or Monarchs.
    • Add crowns, castles and thrones for a regal image.
    • Gold, purple and deep red are the classic royal colors.
    • Mix in archery words like Archers, Bullseyes or Arrow Slingers to tie the name to the sport.

    Royal words that fit archery

    Royal names mix a describing word with a archery word. Try describing words like Silver, Honored, Valiant, Majestic, Regal, Gallant, Sovereign, Royal, Golden, Ivory, next to archery words like Target Takers, Straight Shooters, Arrow Slingers, Archers, Longbows, Quiver Crew, Robin Hoods, Draw Masters, Bowmen, Fletchers. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
