    About IndyCar

    IndyCar races single-seaters on ovals, road courses and street circuits, and its crown jewel is the Indianapolis 500. Teams run one to five cars and win the entrants' championship as well as the drivers' title. The series draws drivers from the United States, New Zealand, Spain, Sweden and Mexico.

    What makes a good IndyCar team name?

    IndyCar is American open-wheel racing, famous for oval speedways and the Indianapolis 500. The best IndyCar team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Indy Racers, Oval Burners or Pace Car Crew, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Indy Racers, Oval Burners or Pace Car Crew, so the name clearly belongs to IndyCar.
    • Say it out loud. A good IndyCar team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A word from the sport itself. For IndyCar that means Indy Racers, Oval Burners or Pace Car Crew, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
