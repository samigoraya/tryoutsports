    What makes a good mythical billiards and pool team name?

    Mythical names draw on dragons, phoenixes, titans and legends. They feel epic and make great logos. For billiards and pool, that means mixing mythical words with the sport itself: Cue Masters, Rack Attackers and Pocket Kings all work well.

    • Pick a creature with a strong silhouette: Dragons, Griffins or Phoenixes.
    • Add a mythic word like Ancient, Immortal, Eternal or Legend.
    • Borrow from the myths of your own region for a local twist.
    • Mix in billiards and pool words like Cue Masters, Rack Attackers or Pocket Kings to tie the name to the sport.

    Mythical words that fit billiards and pool

    Mythical names mix a describing word with a billiards and pool word. Try describing words like Moonlit, Arcane, Celestial, Sacred, Elder, Flame, Golden, Emerald, Frost, Crystal, next to billiards and pool words like Sharks, Eight Ballers, Table Runners, Cue Masters, Nine Ballers, Pocket Kings, Chalk Crew, Bank Shots, Corner Pockets, English Spinners. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
