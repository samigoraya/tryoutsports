    What makes a good royal road cycling team name?

    Royal names borrow crowns, castles and noble titles. They sound proud and look sharp on gold-trimmed kit. For road cycling, that means mixing royal words with the sport itself: Peloton, Breakaways and Climbers all work well.

    • Use titles like Kings, Queens, Barons, Knights or Monarchs.
    • Add crowns, castles and thrones for a regal image.
    • Gold, purple and deep red are the classic royal colors.
    • Mix in road cycling words like Peloton, Breakaways or Climbers to tie the name to the sport.

    Royal words that fit road cycling

    Royal names mix a describing word with a road cycling word. Try describing words like Crowned, Ivory, Majestic, Regal, Grand, Exalted, Gilded, Velvet, Supreme, Imperial, next to road cycling words like Wheel Suckers, Domestiques, Peloton, Yellow Jerseys, Gear Grinders, Echelon Crew, Time Trialists, Sprinters, Pace Line, Climbers. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
