    What makes a good fierce korfball team name?

    Fierce names borrow from predators, storms and heavy metal. They are short, hard-sounding and built to be chanted. For korfball, that means mixing fierce words with the sport itself: Korfers, Shooters and Rebounders all work well.

    • Pick predators with a strong sound: Vipers, Wolves, Raptors or Sharks.
    • Add weather for power: Thunder, Cyclone, Avalanche or Storm.
    • Use one hard word when you want an intimidating name that is easy to chant.
    • Mix in korfball words like Korfers, Shooters or Rebounders to tie the name to the sport.

    Fierce words that fit korfball

    Fierce names mix a describing word with a korfball word. Try describing words like Red, Storm, Shadow, Lightning, Savage, Crimson, Midnight, Steel, Fire, Howling, next to korfball words like Shooters, Team Players, Basket Builders, Attackers, Tall Posts, Zone Switchers, Dutch Masters, Basket Hunters, Defenders, Runners. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
