    What makes a good mythical beach tennis team name?

    Mythical names draw on dragons, phoenixes, titans and legends. They feel epic and make great logos. For beach tennis, that means mixing mythical words with the sport itself: Sand Volleyers, Sun Smashers and Barefoot Brigade all work well.

    • Pick a creature with a strong silhouette: Dragons, Griffins or Phoenixes.
    • Add a mythic word like Ancient, Immortal, Eternal or Legend.
    • Borrow from the myths of your own region for a local twist.
    • Mix in beach tennis words like Sand Volleyers, Sun Smashers or Barefoot Brigade to tie the name to the sport.
