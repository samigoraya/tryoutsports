    What makes a good classic baseball team name?

    Classic names borrow from the early days of the sport: trades, travel and the ballpark itself. They are short, easy to say and look good stitched across a jersey.

    • Pair a town with a trade or tradition, like Millers, Foresters or Railmen.
    • Use ballpark words such as Sluggers, Southpaws, Knuckleballers or Line Drives.
    • Keep it to two words so it fits a jersey front and a scoreboard.
    • Add your hometown at the end for a clean, traditional look.

    Questions people ask

    What is a good classic name for a baseball team?

    Names built on trades, travel or the game itself tend to age well, for example Barnstormers, Pioneers or Double Plays. Fill in your hometown above to see them next to your own city.

    How long should a classic baseball team name be?

    One to three words. Two words fit best on a uniform and read clearly on a scoreboard.

    Can I use these classic names for my team?

    Yes. The names are random combinations, so check that nobody in your league already uses the same one before you order uniforms.

    How do I check that a team name is free to use?

    Search the name with your town and sport, read your league's naming rules, and see whether the social handle and web address are free. If you plan to sell merchandise, search your country's trademark register as well. The generator does not check any of this for you.
