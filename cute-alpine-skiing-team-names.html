    What makes a good cute alpine skiing team name?

    Cute names use baby animals, sweets and sunshine. They are warm, easy for kids to say and easy to draw. For alpine skiing, that means mixing cute words with the sport itself: Downhillers, Slalom Skiers and Gate Crashers all work well.

    • Choose animals kids love: Bunnies, Ducklings, Pandas or Puppies.
    • Add a friendly word like Happy, Sunny, Fluffy or Tiny.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw so kids can doodle it.
    • Mix in alpine skiing words like Downhillers, Slalom Skiers or Gate Crashers to tie the name to the sport.
