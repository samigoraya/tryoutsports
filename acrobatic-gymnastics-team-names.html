    About acrobatic gymnastics

    Acrobatic gymnastics is built entirely on partnership: bases lift, throw and balance tops in pairs, women's groups of three and men's groups of four. Routines are judged on balance, dynamics and artistry. Russia, China, Belgium, Great Britain, Portugal and Israel lead the sport.

    • Team sizePairs of 2, groups of 3 or 4

    What makes a good acrobatic gymnastics team name?

    Acrobatic gymnastics features partner and group balances, lifts and tumbling to music. The best acrobatic gymnastics team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Bases, Tops or Balance Crew, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Bases, Tops or Balance Crew, so the name clearly belongs to acrobatic gymnastics.
    • Say it out loud. A good acrobatic gymnastics team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.
    • Match the tone to the team: fierce for competitive squads, funny or cute for social and youth teams.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Alliance, Syndicate, Collective or Brigade. Try your town plus Crew for a acrobatic gymnastics side.
    • A word from the sport itself. For acrobatic gymnastics that means Bases, Tops or Balance Crew, which tells everyone what you play.
