    What makes a good spooky supercross team name?

    Spooky names use ghosts, graveyards and midnight. They are playful rather than scary, and great for Halloween leagues. For supercross, that means mixing spooky words with the sport itself: Stadium Riders, Rhythm Masters and Triple Crown all work well.

    • Try ghosts, ghouls, bats and phantoms for a classic spooky feel.
    • Add time words like Midnight, Moonlit or Haunted.
    • Black, purple and orange give a Halloween look.
    • Mix in supercross words like Stadium Riders, Rhythm Masters or Triple Crown to tie the name to the sport.

    Spooky words that fit supercross

    Spooky names mix a describing word with a supercross word. Try describing words like Hollow, Howling, Cursed, Phantom, Foggy, Grim, Wicked, Spooky, Ghostly, Midnight, next to supercross words like Rhythm Masters, Stadium Kings, Jump Squad, Stadium Riders, Dirt Flyers, Mud Crew, Triple Crown, Whoop Crew, Holeshot Kings. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
