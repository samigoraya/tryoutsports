    About bowling

    Ten-pin bowling is almost always played as a league team sport, with squads of three to five rolling two games a week through a season. A perfect game is twelve strikes for 300. The United States, South Korea, Sweden, Finland and Malaysia lead the world championships.

    What makes a good bowling team name?

    Bowling is a league favourite where players roll a ball at ten pins. The best bowling team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Strikes, Spares or Turkeys, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Strikes, Spares or Turkeys, so the name clearly belongs to bowling.
    • Say it out loud. A good bowling team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Alliance, Syndicate, Collective or Brigade. Try your town plus Crew for a bowling side.
    • A word from the sport itself. For bowling that means Strikes, Spares or Turkeys, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
