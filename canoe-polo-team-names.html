    About canoe polo

    Canoe polo, also called kayak polo, is a ball sport played by five-a-side teams in kayaks, passing by hand or paddle and shooting at a goal suspended two metres above the water. It is fast, physical and played in pools and open water. Germany, Great Britain, Italy, France, Spain and the Netherlands are the strongest nations.

    • Team size5 a side in kayaks
    • Biggest eventsICF Canoe Polo World Championships

    What makes a good canoe polo team name?

    Canoe polo is a team ball game played in kayaks, with goals suspended over the water. The best canoe polo team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Polo Paddlers, Kayak Passers or Roll Masters, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Polo Paddlers, Kayak Passers or Roll Masters, so the name clearly belongs to canoe polo.
    • Say it out loud. A good canoe polo team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Match the tone to the team: fierce for competitive squads, funny or cute for social and youth teams.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers, Alliance, Athletic or Sporting. Columbus Crew and Philadelphia Union are built this way, and it works for any sport. Try your town plus Crew for a canoe polo side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For canoe polo that means Polo Paddlers, Kayak Passers or Roll Masters, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
