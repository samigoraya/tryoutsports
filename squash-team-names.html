    About squash

    Squash is played inside a four-walled court with a small rubber ball, and rallies continue off any wall. Club and national team events string several singles matches together. Egypt leads the sport today, with England, Pakistan, Malaysia, France and the United States also strong.

    • Team size1 in singles, with teams of 3 to 5 in leagues
    • Biggest eventsWorld Squash Championships, World Team Squash Championships
    • Sport groupRacquet & Paddle Sports

    What makes a good squash team name?

    Squash is played in an enclosed court where players take turns hitting a small rubber ball against the front wall. The best squash team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Wall Hitters, Boasts or Drop Shots, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Wall Hitters, Boasts or Drop Shots, so the name clearly belongs to squash.
    • Say it out loud. A good squash team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A word from the sport itself. For squash that means Wall Hitters, Boasts or Drop Shots, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
