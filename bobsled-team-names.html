    About bobsled

    Bobsleigh sends crews of two or four down an iced track at over 130km/h, with the pilot steering and the brakeman stopping. The push start is often where the race is won. Germany dominates, with Switzerland, Latvia, the United States, Canada and Italy also strong.

    What makes a good bobsled team name?

    Bobsled is a high-speed sliding sport where teams push, jump in and steer down an ice track. The best bobsled team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Bobsledders, Brakemen or Pushers, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Bobsledders, Brakemen or Pushers, so the name clearly belongs to bobsled.
    • Say it out loud. A good bobsled team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Alliance, Syndicate, Collective or Brigade. Try your town plus Crew for a bobsled side.
    • A word from the sport itself. For bobsled that means Bobsledders, Brakemen or Pushers, which tells everyone what you play.
