    About paragliding

    Paragliding flies a soft wing, using thermals to climb and glide for hundreds of kilometres, scored in cross-country and accuracy events. World meets add the scores of a national team of three to five pilots. France, Switzerland, Austria, Slovenia, Spain and Brazil lead the sport.

    What makes a good paragliding team name?

    Paragliding is a soaring sport under a lightweight, foot-launched wing. The best paragliding team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Paragliders, Canopy Pilots or Thermal Chasers, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Paragliders, Canopy Pilots or Thermal Chasers, so the name clearly belongs to paragliding.
    • Say it out loud. A good paragliding team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.
    • Pick a mascot that is easy to draw, since it will end up on shirts, flags and social media.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
    • A word from the sport itself. For paragliding that means Paragliders, Canopy Pilots or Thermal Chasers, which tells everyone what you play.
