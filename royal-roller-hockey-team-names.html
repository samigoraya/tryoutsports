    What makes a good royal roller hockey team name?

    Royal names borrow crowns, castles and noble titles. They sound proud and look sharp on gold-trimmed kit. For roller hockey, that means mixing royal words with the sport itself: Rollers, Wheelers and Skaters all work well.

    • Use titles like Kings, Queens, Barons, Knights or Monarchs.
    • Add crowns, castles and thrones for a regal image.
    • Gold, purple and deep red are the classic royal colors.
    • Mix in roller hockey words like Rollers, Wheelers or Skaters to tie the name to the sport.
