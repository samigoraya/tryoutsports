    What makes a good classic skydiving team name?

    Classic names lean on tradition, trades and hometown pride. They are short, timeless and look good stitched across a jersey. For skydiving, that means mixing classic words with the sport itself: Skydivers, Freefall Crew and Canopy Crew all work well.

    • Pair your town with a trade or tradition, like Millers, Pioneers or Railmen.
    • Keep it to two words so it fits a jersey and reads clearly on a scoreboard.
    • Pick a name that will still sound good in ten years.
    • Mix in skydiving words like Skydivers, Freefall Crew or Canopy Crew to tie the name to the sport.

    Classic words that fit skydiving

    Classic names mix a describing word with a skydiving word. Try describing words like Diamond, Summer, Copper, Royal, Evening, Iron, Union, Golden, River, Silver, next to skydiving words like Wingsuit Flyers, Sky Surfers, Canopy Crew, Chute Crew, Formation Crew, Skydivers, Freefall Crew. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
