    What makes a good classic MMA team name?

    Classic names lean on tradition, trades and hometown pride. They are short, timeless and look good stitched across a jersey. For MMA, that means mixing classic words with the sport itself: Cage Fighters, Octagon Kings and Strikers all work well.

    • Pair your town with a trade or tradition, like Millers, Pioneers or Railmen.
    • Keep it to two words so it fits a jersey and reads clearly on a scoreboard.
    • Pick a name that will still sound good in ten years.
    • Mix in MMA words like Cage Fighters, Octagon Kings or Strikers to tie the name to the sport.

    Classic words that fit MMA

    Classic names mix a describing word with a MMA word. Try describing words like Dusty, Copper, Golden, Summer, Evening, Union, Mountain, Royal, Iron, Silver, next to MMA words like Cage Fighters, Fight Night, Submission Artists, Finishers, Strikers, Knockout Kings, Takedown Masters, Octagon Kings, Grapplers, Cage Warriors. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
