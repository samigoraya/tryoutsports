    What makes a good space bodyboarding team name?

    Space names borrow rockets, planets and galaxies. They sound fast and futuristic and look great with metallic colors. For bodyboarding, that means mixing space words with the sport itself: Body Boarders, Drop Knee Riders and Barrel Cruisers all work well.

    • Use planets and objects: Comets, Meteors, Rockets or Nebulas.
    • Add a futuristic word like Cosmic, Stellar, Orbital or Quantum.
    • Silver, navy and electric blue match the theme.
    • Mix in bodyboarding words like Body Boarders, Drop Knee Riders or Barrel Cruisers to tie the name to the sport.
