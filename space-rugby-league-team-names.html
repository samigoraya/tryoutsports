    What makes a good space rugby league team name?

    Space names borrow rockets, planets and galaxies. They sound fast and futuristic and look great with metallic colors. For rugby league, that means mixing space words with the sport itself: Tacklers, Play The Balls and Hit Ups all work well.

    • Use planets and objects: Comets, Meteors, Rockets or Nebulas.
    • Add a futuristic word like Cosmic, Stellar, Orbital or Quantum.
    • Silver, navy and electric blue match the theme.
    • Mix in rugby league words like Tacklers, Play The Balls or Hit Ups to tie the name to the sport.

    Space words that fit rugby league

    Space names mix a describing word with a rugby league word. Try describing words like Orbital, Sonic, Star, Galactic, Future, Neon, Turbo, Hyper, Lunar, Nova, next to rugby league words like Offloads, Locks, Kick Chasers, Dummy Halves, Tryscorers, Backs, Forwards, Scrums, Tacklers, Hit Ups. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
