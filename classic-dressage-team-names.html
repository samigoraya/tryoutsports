    What makes a good classic dressage team name?

    Classic names lean on tradition, trades and hometown pride. They are short, timeless and look good stitched across a jersey. For dressage, that means mixing classic words with the sport itself: Piaffe Pros, Pirouettes and Passage Crew all work well.

    • Pair your town with a trade or tradition, like Millers, Pioneers or Railmen.
    • Keep it to two words so it fits a jersey and reads clearly on a scoreboard.
    • Pick a name that will still sound good in ten years.
    • Mix in dressage words like Piaffe Pros, Pirouettes or Passage Crew to tie the name to the sport.
