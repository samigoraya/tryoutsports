    About artistic gymnastics

    Artistic gymnastics is judged on difficulty and execution across six apparatus for men and four for women. Team finals add the best scores from a squad of four or five, so club and national teams train as a unit. The United States, China, Japan, Russia, Great Britain and Italy are the strongest nations.

    • Team size1 gymnast, with teams of 4 or 5
    • Biggest eventsOlympic Games, World Artistic Gymnastics Championships
    • Sport groupGymnastics & Acrobatics

    What makes a good artistic gymnastics team name?

    Artistic gymnastics includes floor, vault, bars and beam, scored on difficulty and execution. The best artistic gymnastics team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Tumblers, Vaulters or Beam Balancers, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Tumblers, Vaulters or Beam Balancers, so the name clearly belongs to artistic gymnastics.

    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Alliance, Syndicate, Collective or Brigade. Try your town plus Crew for a artistic gymnastics side.
    • A word from the sport itself. For artistic gymnastics that means Tumblers, Vaulters or Beam Balancers, which tells everyone what you play.
