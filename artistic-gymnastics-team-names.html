    About artistic gymnastics

    Artistic gymnastics is judged on difficulty and execution across six apparatus for men and four for women. Team finals add the best scores from a squad of four or five, so club and national teams train as a unit. The United States, China, Japan, Russia, Great Britain and Italy are the strongest nations.

    • Team size1 gymnast, with teams of 4 or 5

    What makes a good artistic gymnastics team name?

    Artistic gymnastics includes floor, vault, bars and beam, scored on difficulty and execution. The best artistic gymnastics team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Tumblers, Vaulters or Beam Balancers, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Tumblers, Vaulters or Beam Balancers, so the name clearly belongs to artistic gymnastics.
    • Say it out loud. A good artistic gymnastics team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers, Alliance, Athletic or Sporting. Columbus Crew and Philadelphia Union are built this way, and it works for any sport. Try your town plus Crew for a artistic gymnastics side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For artistic gymnastics that means Tumblers, Vaulters or Beam Balancers, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
