    About ultra marathon

    Ultra running covers anything beyond the marathon, from 50km road races to 160km mountain trails and multi-day events. Many ultras score club teams, and relay ultras split the distance between four to six runners. South Africa, France, the United States, Japan, Great Britain and Spain have the biggest ultra scenes.

    What makes a good ultra marathon team name?

    Ultra marathons are races longer than the standard marathon, often on trails and mountains. The best ultra marathon team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Ultra Runners, Trail Crew or Mountain Crew, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers, Alliance, Athletic or Sporting. Columbus Crew and Philadelphia Union are built this way, and it works for any sport. Try your town plus Crew for a ultra marathon side.
    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A local trade or landmark. Steelers, Brewers, Miners, Mariners and Railmen all come from the work a town was known for.
    • A word from the sport itself. For ultra marathon that means Ultra Runners, Trail Crew or Mountain Crew, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
