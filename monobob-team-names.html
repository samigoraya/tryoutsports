    About monobob

    The monobob is a single-seat bobsleigh, introduced to give women their own solo sliding event at the 2022 Winter Olympics. Everyone races a standard sled, so driving and the push start decide it. The United States, Germany, Australia, Canada and Switzerland have been the strongest nations.

    What makes a good monobob team name?

    Monobob is a one-person bobsled event, steered, pushed and piloted by the athlete alone. The best monobob team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Monobobbers, Solo Sliders or Single Seaters, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Monobobbers, Solo Sliders or Single Seaters, so the name clearly belongs to monobob.
    • Say it out loud. A good monobob team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • One collective word works on its own: Crew, Union, Dynamo, Rovers or Alliance, with your town in front.
    • Add your hometown or neighborhood to make the name yours.
    • Make a shortlist of five and let the team vote.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Alliance, Syndicate, Collective or Brigade. Try your town plus Crew for a monobob side.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
    • A word from the sport itself. For monobob that means Monobobbers, Solo Sliders or Single Seaters, which tells everyone what you play.
