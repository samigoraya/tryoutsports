    What makes a good fierce Para-Cycling team name?

    Fierce names borrow from predators, storms and heavy metal. They are short, hard-sounding and built to be chanted. For Para-Cycling, that means mixing fierce words with the sport itself: Handcyclers, Tandem Crew and Trike Racers all work well.

    • Pick predators with a strong sound: Vipers, Wolves, Raptors or Sharks.
    • Add weather for power: Thunder, Cyclone, Avalanche or Storm.
    • Use one hard word when you want an intimidating name that is easy to chant.
    • Mix in Para-Cycling words like Handcyclers, Tandem Crew or Trike Racers to tie the name to the sport.

    Fierce words that fit Para-Cycling

    Fierce names mix a describing word with a Para-Cycling word. Try describing words like Lightning, Howling, Night, Shadow, Thunder, Storm, Fighting, Savage, Fire, Desert, next to Para-Cycling words like Pedal Warriors, Tandem Crew, Wheel Warriors, Adaptive Riders, Cycle Heroes, Para Power, Speed Spinners, Crank Crew, Trike Racers, Handcyclers. Keep it to two or three words so it fits a jersey.
