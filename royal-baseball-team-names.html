    What makes a good royal baseball team name?

    Royal names borrow from kings, knights and castles. They sound proud and look sharp in gold, purple or silver.

    • Use ranks and titles: Kings, Dukes, Barons, Regents, Sovereigns.
    • Borrow castle words: Crowns, Thrones, Citadels, Banners, Crests.
    • Add Royal, Golden or Noble in front when you want extra weight.
    • Match the name with gold, purple or silver in your uniform design.
