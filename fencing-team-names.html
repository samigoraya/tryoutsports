    About fencing

    Fencing is fought with the foil, epee or sabre on a narrow piste, with hits registered electronically. Team events use a relay of three fencers to 45 points. Italy, France, Hungary, Russia, South Korea, the United States, Japan and China are the strongest nations.

    • Team size1 fencer, with team events of 3 plus a reserve
    • Biggest eventsOlympic Games, World Fencing Championships
    • Sport groupCombat Sports & Martial Arts

    What makes a good fencing team name?

    Fencing is a sword sport of speed, precision and tactics, with foil, epee and sabre disciplines. The best fencing team names usually borrow from the sport itself, such as Foils, Epees or Sabres, then add a place, a color or a strong describing word.

    • Use the sport's own words, like Foils, Epees or Sabres, so the name clearly belongs to fencing.
    • Say it out loud. A good fencing team name is easy to chant and easy to read on a jersey.
    • Match the tone to the team: fierce for competitive squads, funny or cute for social and youth teams.
    • Check that nobody in your league already uses the name before you order uniforms.
    • Keep it to one to three words so it fits a uniform, a banner and a scoreboard.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • A plural animal or mascot. Easy to chant and easy to draw on a shirt: Wolves, Vipers, Falcons, Bulls.
    • A word from the sport itself. For fencing that means Foils, Epees or Sabres, which tells everyone what you play.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
