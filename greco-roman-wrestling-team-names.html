    About Greco-Roman wrestling

    Greco-Roman wrestling bans attacks below the waist, so everything happens through upper-body throws, locks and lifts. It has been on the Olympic programme since the first modern Games in 1896. Russia, Iran, Turkey, Cuba, Hungary and Georgia have the deepest traditions.

    How real teams choose a name

    Look down the list above and the same patterns keep coming back. Plenty of famous teams are just one collective word, like Crew, Union, United, Dynamo or Rovers, put behind a city name. Others use a plural animal, a local trade, or a word taken straight from the sport.

    • One collective word. A single word for the whole group: Crew, Union, Alliance, Syndicate, Collective or Brigade. Try your town plus Crew for a Greco-Roman wrestling side.
    • A colour or kit. Reds, Blues, Greens and Blacks have named clubs for over a century, and they match your uniform without any thought.
    • A word from the sport itself. For Greco-Roman wrestling that means Gut Wrenchers, Body Lockers or Suplexers, which tells everyone what you play.
